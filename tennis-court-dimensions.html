Tennis court dimensions

The regulation tennis court for singles and doubles, the service boxes, the net and the run-off space around it, in feet and metres.

Quick answer

A tennis court is 78 ft long (23.77 m). It is 36 ft wide for doubles (10.97 m) and 27 ft for singles (8.23 m). The service line is 21 ft (6.40 m) from the net. The net is 3 ft (0.914 m) high at the centre and 3 ft 6 in (1.07 m) at the posts. With run-off, a court needs about 120 × 60 ft (36.6 × 18.3 m).

Last updated 2026-11-25

Drawn to scale (metres). Inner long lines: singles sidelines. The doubles alleys are 1.37 m (4 ft 6 in) wide.

From the ITF Rules of Tennis. The run-off figures are the ITF's recommended minimums for club and recreational courts.

How big is a tennis court in square metres?

The doubles court itself is about 261 m² (2,808 sq ft). With the recommended run-off it's about 669 m² (7,200 sq ft), which is the figure to use when planning a court or a hall.

Tennis vs padel

A padel court is 20 × 10 m inside glass walls, about three quarters of a tennis doubles court and with no run-off, so one padel court fits on a standard tennis plot with its run-off (36.6 × 18.3 m), or three side by side if the plot is widened to 20.8 m. Questions players ask

What are the dimensions of a tennis court?

78 ft long (23.77 m); 36 ft wide for doubles (10.97 m) and 27 ft for singles (8.23 m).

How high is a tennis net?

3 ft (0.914 m) at the centre and 3 ft 6 in (1.07 m) at the posts.

How far is the service line from the net?

21 ft (6.40 m). The service line is 18 ft (5.49 m) from the baseline.

How much space do I need for a tennis court?

About 120 × 60 ft (36.6 × 18.3 m) including the recommended run-off around the lines.

How wide is the doubles alley?

4 ft 6 in (1.37 m) on each side.
