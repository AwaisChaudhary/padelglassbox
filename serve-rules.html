Was that serve legal?

Answer a few quick questions about the serve and get the call: good serve, fault or let. Then read every padel serve rule in plain English below.

Quick answer

Stand behind the service line, bounce the ball once on your side, and hit it at or below waist height with at least one foot on the ground. It must land in the service box diagonally opposite (lines are in). You get two serves. A serve that clips the net and lands in the box is a let (replayed). A serve that bounces in and then hits the metal fence is a fault.

How do you serve in padel, step by step?

1. Stand in the right place

Stand behind the service line, between the centre line and the side wall. Your feet must not touch the service line or the imaginary line that extends the centre line.

2. Bounce the ball

Drop the ball and let it bounce once on your side, behind the service line, before you hit it. You may not hit it straight from your hand.

3. Hit at or below the waist

Contact must be at or below waist height, so you serve underarm or side-arm, never overhead. Keep at least one foot on the ground: no jump serves.

4. Land it diagonally

The ball must cross the net and bounce in the service box diagonally opposite you. A ball that lands on the line is good.

5. The receiver waits for the bounce

The receiver must let the serve bounce before playing it. After the bounce the ball may hit the glass and stay in play.

6. Two tries

A fault on the first serve gives you a second serve. Two faults in a row is a double fault, and the receivers win the point.

Who serves first in padel, and when do you switch?

Toss for the first serve

Before the match, a coin toss (or spin of a racket) decides. The winners choose to serve, receive or pick an end, or let the other pair choose.

Set the order for each set

Before each set, each pair decides which partner serves first, and keeps that order for the set. The receiving pair also decides who receives on which side in the first game of the set, and keeps it.

A whole game each

One player serves for the whole game. Then the other pair serves the next game, and so on.

Swap sides every point

The server changes side after every point, so each point is served diagonally to the other receiver.

Golden point and Star Point

On a deciding point the receiving pair chooses which player receives. See how deciding points work.

First point from the right

Each game starts with a serve from the right-hand side of the court, then the server switches sides every point.

Time between points

FIP rules allow up to 20 seconds between points, 90 seconds when you change ends, and 2 minutes between sets.

Questions players ask

Can you serve overarm in padel?

No. You must hit the ball at or below waist height after it bounces, so every padel serve is underhand.

Is it a let if the serve hits the net in padel?

Yes, if it then lands in the correct box and does not touch the metal fence before bouncing again. If it touches the net and lands out, it is a fault.

What if the serve hits the fence in padel?

If the ball bounces in the box and then hits the metal fence before a second bounce, it is a fault. If it hits the glass instead, the ball is in play.

Does the serve have to bounce in padel?

Yes. The server must bounce the ball behind the service line before hitting it, and the receiver must let the serve bounce before returning it.

Can you stand on the line when serving in padel?

No. Touching the service line or the imaginary extension of the centre line with your foot when you serve is a fault.

Can you jump when you serve in padel?

No. At least one foot must be on the ground when you hit the ball.

How many serves do you get in padel?

Two. If the first is a fault, you have a second serve. Two faults is a double fault and loses the point.

Who serves first in padel?

A coin toss decides. The winning pair chooses to serve or receive, or picks an end. Before each set, each pair also decides which partner serves first.

How long do you have between points in padel?

Under FIP rules, up to 20 seconds between points, 90 seconds when you change ends and 2 minutes between sets.

Which side do you serve from first in padel?

From the right-hand side. The server then switches sides after every point for the rest of the game.
