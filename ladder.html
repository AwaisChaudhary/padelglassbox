Run a challenge ladder for your club or group. Add your pairs or players, record each challenge and the ladder reorders itself, with win and loss records, movement arrows and a full match history. Free, no sign-up, saved on this device.

Quick answer

In a padel ladder, everyone is ranked in a list. You challenge someone a few places above you (often up to 2 or 3). If you win, you take their place; if you lose, nothing changes. Agree the challenge range, the match format and how long people have to accept a challenge before you start.

    How does a padel ladder work?

    A ranked list

    Every pair (or player) has a place on the ladder. The starting order can be by level, by last season, or simply random. Use the level calculator if you want a fair first order.

    Challenge upwards

    You can only challenge someone above you, and usually only a few places above. That keeps matches close and stops the bottom team taking on the top team every week.

    Win and climb

    Beat a higher team and you move up. The two common ways: swap places with them, or take their spot and push everyone in between down one. Lose and the ladder stays the same.

    No fixed end

    A ladder runs all season. Players arrange their own matches when it suits them, which makes it a good fit for busy clubs.

    Pairs or singles

    Padel is a doubles game, so most ladders are for fixed pairs. A single-player ladder works if each match is two ladder players against two others, or if you mix partners.

    Keep score properly

    Agree golden point or advantage first, and keep score on a phone with the score keeper.

    Ladder rules to agree before you start

    Challenge range

    How many places up can you challenge? 2 or 3 is common. A bigger range lets new strong pairs climb faster.

    Time to accept

    Set a limit, for example 7 to 14 days to play a challenge. If the challenged pair won't play in time, the challenger can be given the match.

    Match format

    Best of three sets, one long set, or a timed match on one court booking. Keep it the same for everyone.

    Rematches

    Many ladders say you can't challenge the same pair again straight away. You must play someone else first.

    Staying active

    Some ladders move a pair down if they don't play for a few weeks, so the ladder stays live.

    Who books and pays

    Usually the challenger books the court and the cost is shared. Write it down so there are no arguments.

    Questions players ask

    What is a padel ladder?

    A ranked list of pairs or players. You challenge someone above you, and if you win you move up. It runs all season and players arrange their own matches.

    How many places can you challenge in a ladder?

    Each ladder sets its own rule. Up to 2 or 3 places above you is common. This tracker lets you pick 2, 3, 5 or anyone above.

    What happens when a challenger wins?

    Either the two swap places, or the winner takes the higher spot and everyone in between moves down one place. Agree which rule you use before you start.

    What happens if the challenger loses?

    In most ladders nothing changes: the challenged pair keeps its place. Some ladders stop the loser challenging the same pair again straight away.

    What is the difference between a ladder and a box league?

    In a ladder you challenge people above you whenever you like. In a box league you play everyone in a small group over a few weeks, then the top moves up a box and the bottom moves down.

    Where is the ladder saved?

    On this device, in your browser. Use 'Copy ladder' to share the rankings in a group chat, and keep updating it on the same phone or computer.
