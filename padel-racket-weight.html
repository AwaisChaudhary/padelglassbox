Padel racket weight calculator: what weight should you get?

Answer six quick questions about your level, strength, arm and style. The calculator gives a weight range in grams to look for on the product page, plus the balance and feel that go with it.

Quick answer

Most adult padel rackets weigh 345 to 385 g. As a rough guide: beginners and players with sore elbows or shoulders do best around 345 to 360 g, most improving players around 355 to 370 g, and strong advanced players who hit hard around 360 to 375 g or more. Juniors need lighter rackets. Weight on the label can be a few grams out, and an overgrip or protector tape adds a little.

Is a heavier or lighter padel racket better?

Feel 1

Lighter

Faster to move at the net and in quick exchanges, easier on the arm and easier to learn with. Less power from the racket itself, and it can twist on off-centre hits.

Feel 2

Heavier

More power and stability on smashes, deep lobs and hard volleys. Slower to swing and more tiring, so late hits and arm strain are more likely if you are not ready for it.

Feel 3

Weight vs balance

A heavy, head-light racket can feel lighter than a lighter head-heavy one. Balance decides how heavy the head feels. Check both numbers.

How do you know if your padel racket is too heavy?

SignWhat it usually means
You are often late on fast volleys at the netToo heavy, or too head-heavy, for your hand speed
Your arm or shoulder is tired by the second setToo heavy for how long and how often you play
Outer elbow pain after playingTry lighter and lower balance, and look at grip tension: padel elbow check
Smashes and lobs feel weak and the racket twistsPossibly too light for how hard you hit

Small changes add up: an overgrip and protector tape each add a few grams, mostly at the handle or the frame. Lead tape on the frame adds weight and moves the balance towards the head, so add it a little at a time.

Questions players ask

What weight padel racket should I get?

Most adults do well between 345 and 375 g. Beginners and players with sore arms should stay around 345 to 360 g; strong, experienced players who hit hard can go to 365 to 375 g or more.

What is a good padel racket weight for beginners?

About 345 to 360 g. A lighter racket is easier to swing, quicker at the net and kinder to your elbow while you learn.

Is 370 g heavy for a padel racket?

It is at the heavier end of normal. Many advanced players use 365 to 375 g, but for beginners or players with elbow pain it is usually too heavy.

Are heavier padel rackets more powerful?

Yes, a heavier racket gives more power and stability if you can swing it at the same speed. If it makes you late or tired, you lose that advantage.

Does an overgrip change the weight of a padel racket?

A little. An overgrip and protector tape each add a few grams. Lead tape adds more and also changes the balance.

What weight padel racket for women?

Pick by strength and arm history, not gender. Many players start around 345 to 360 g; strong, experienced players can go heavier.
