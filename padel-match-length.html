How long is a padel match? Match length calculator

Pick the format, the deuce rule and your level. The calculator plays the match 2,000 times with real padel scoring and rest times, and tells you how long it usually takes, and if it fits your court booking.

Quick answer

A club padel match of best of 3 sets usually takes about 60 to 90 minutes. Two sets with a super tie-break instead of a third set takes about 45 to 70 minutes, and one set about 20 to 35 minutes. A study of Italian second-division matches measured an average of 54 minutes, with only about a third of that time spent actually rallying. Pro matches often take 90 minutes or more.

What makes a padel match longer or shorter?

The deuce rule

Advantage scoring adds about 10% to a match compared with golden point, because games at 40-40 can go on. Star Point sits in between: two advantages, then one deciding point.

A super tie-break

Playing a tie-break to 10 instead of a third set saves 15 to 30 minutes. That is why leagues and club tournaments love it. See tie-break rules.

Level

Better players have longer rallies. Pro points last about 10 to 15 seconds, club rallies nearer 7. Beginners have short rallies but spend time picking up balls.

How even the pairs are

Even pairs mean more deuces, more tie-breaks and more third sets. A one-sided match can be over in under 45 minutes.

Rest and chat

Under FIP rules you get 20 seconds between points, 90 at changes of ends and 2 minutes between sets. At clubs, slow ball-collecting often adds more.

Warm-up

The FIP warm-up is usually 3 to 5 minutes. At clubs, agree it first: a 10-minute warm-up eats a big part of a 60-minute booking.

Questions players ask

How long does a padel match last?

A club match of best of 3 sets usually takes about 60 to 90 minutes. Two sets plus a super tie-break takes about 45 to 70 minutes, and one set about 20 to 35 minutes. A study of Italian second-division matches measured 54 minutes on average.

How long is a professional padel match?

Pro matches on the Premier Padel tour often take 90 minutes to 2 hours, because points are longer (about 10 to 15 seconds) and many matches go to three sets.

How long is one set of padel?

Usually about 20 to 35 minutes for club players, depending on how close it is and whether you play golden point or advantage.

Is 90 minutes enough for a padel match?

Usually yes. A 90-minute booking fits a best-of-3 match with golden point and a short warm-up most of the time. With advantage scoring and two even pairs, a third set can push you past 90 minutes, so play a super tie-break instead.

How much of a padel match is actual play?

Only about a third. In the Italian study, 31% of the match was ball in play; the rest was time between points, changes of ends and breaks.

How long is a padel game?

One game (to 4 points) usually takes about 2 to 3 minutes for club players, longer at 40-40 with advantage scoring.
