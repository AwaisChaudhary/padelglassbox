Premier Padel 2027 calendar: what is confirmed so far.

The pro padel season, in one place. We list only dates that the organisers have announced, and we update this page when the full 2027 tour is published.

Quick answer

The full Premier Padel 2027 calendar has not been published yet. The 2025 calendar came out on 23 December 2024 and the 2026 one on 18 December 2025, so the 2027 list is expected in mid to late December 2026. Already confirmed: the Hexagon Cup Madrid, 21 to 24 January 2027, which opens the year, and a Miami P1 in March 2027 at the Miami Beach Convention Center.

Hexagon is a team competition (eight teams) run under FIP. It is separate from the Premier Padel ranking tournaments, but it sits on the same calendar.

How the Premier Padel tour works

Four tiers

Majors are the top events (four a season, 2,000 points for the winners), then P1 and P2 tournaments. The season ends with the Finals for the top eight pairs. See padel rankings explained.

How big is the tour?

The 2026 tour had 26 tournaments in 18 countries, with about three in four played indoors. It opened with the Riyadh P1 on 9 February and added London and Pretoria.

What changes in 2027?

FIP has said players will get more ranking points for the opening rounds of Majors, P1 and P2 events from 2027, to reward progress through the draw.

Before 2027 starts

The 2026 season ends with the Premier Padel Finals in Barcelona, 10 to 13 December 2026. The last Major of the year was in Acapulco: see Premier Padel Mexico Major 2026. New to watching? Questions players ask

When will the Premier Padel 2027 calendar be released?

It has not been published yet. The last two calendars came out in December (23 December 2024 and 18 December 2025), so mid to late December 2026 is likely, but no date has been given.

When does the 2027 padel season start?

The first confirmed pro event of 2027 is the Hexagon Cup Madrid, 21 to 24 January 2027, at the Caja Mágica. The first Premier Padel ranking event will be known when the calendar is published.

Is there a Premier Padel event in Miami in 2027?

Yes. The Miami Premier Padel P1 is set for March 2027 at the Miami Beach Convention Center, according to the event's official site. Exact days are still to be confirmed.

How many tournaments are on the Premier Padel tour?

The 2026 tour had 26 tournaments in 18 countries. The 2027 number will be confirmed with the calendar.

Where can I watch Premier Padel?

In 2026, Red Bull TV showed every event from the quarter-finals on, and earlier rounds were on Premier Padel's YouTube channel. We will update this for 2027 when broadcasters are confirmed.
