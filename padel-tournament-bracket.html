Padel tournament bracket generator (knockout).

Type in your pairs, pick seeding and courts, and get a single-elimination draw with byes placed for you. Tap the winner of each match to move them on. It works out how long the event will take. Free, no app, saved on this device.

Quick answer

A knockout with N pairs always has N - 1 matches (plus one if you play a 3rd-place match). 8 pairs = 7 matches over 3 rounds; 12 pairs = 11 matches over 4 rounds with 4 byes; 16 pairs = 15 matches over 4 rounds. Byes go to the top seeds so the draw fills up to the next 4, 8, 16 or 32.

How long does a padel match last?

Club best of 3

About 50 to 90 minutes

A study of official Italian sub-elite men's matches measured an average of about 54 minutes per match (Ungureanu and others, 2022), and the same paper puts elite matches at about 50 minutes. A retailer guide from Zona de Padel puts a typical three-set match at about 90 minutes and one set at about 18 minutes. Book 90 minutes of court time for a best-of-3 match.

Rallies

Under 10 seconds a point

In professional World Padel Tour matches the average rally lasted about 9.4 seconds (Courel-Ibáñez and others, 2017). In the Italian sub-elite study it was about 6.7 seconds, with about 15 seconds' rest between rallies. Only about a third of match time is actual play.

Planning

Short formats for big draws

For a one-night knockout, one set or a race to 4 games keeps rounds moving. Add 5 minutes per match for changeovers and warm-up. Want everyone to play all night? Use an Americano or a round robin instead: no one is knocked out.

Our time estimate in the tool uses your format's typical length plus 5 minutes per match, round by round across your courts. Real matches vary a lot with level and golden point vs advantage (golden point explained).

Questions players ask

How many matches are in a padel knockout tournament?

One less than the number of pairs: 8 pairs play 7 matches, 16 pairs play 15. Add one if you play a 3rd-place match.

How do byes work in a padel bracket?

If the number of pairs is not 4, 8, 16 or 32, some teams skip round 1. Byes fill the draw up to the next size and go to the top seeds. 12 pairs in a 16 draw means 4 byes.

How long does a padel match last?

A best-of-3 club match is usually about 50 to 90 minutes. A study of Italian sub-elite matches measured about 54 minutes on average. One set takes roughly 20 to 40 minutes.

How long does a padel tournament take?

Count the rounds, then how many matches each round needs per court. 8 pairs on 2 courts with one-set matches is 4 match slots, or about 2 hours 20 minutes with changeovers. The tool above works it out for you.

Should I seed a padel tournament?

Seed if you know the levels, so the two strongest pairs can only meet in the final. Use a random draw for social events or when no one knows the levels.
