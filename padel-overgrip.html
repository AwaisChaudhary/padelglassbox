How often should you change your padel overgrip?

A fresh overgrip stops the racket turning in your hand and costs less than a can of balls. Use the calculator to see how often to change yours, how many you need a year and what it costs, then check the signs that it is worn out.

Quick answer

Change your padel overgrip about every 8 to 12 hours of play, which is roughly once a week if you play 2 or 3 times a week. Change it sooner if it feels slippery when dry, stays damp or tears. Heavy sweat, heat and humidity can halve its life, and competitive players often fit a new one every 2 or 3 matches or before each match. Even if you play rarely, change it at least every two months.

Overgrip vs replacement grip

OvergripReplacement (base) grip
What it isA thin wrap that goes on top of the gripThe thicker, cushioned grip under it, fixed to the handle
JobTack, sweat and a little thicknessShape, cushion and handle size
Change itEvery 8 to 12 hours of play, or soonerEvery 2 to 3 months if you play several times a week; about once a season if you play now and then
Weight addedAbout 8 g eachAlready counted in the racket's weight

Want a thicker handle? One overgrip builds it a little; two make it clearly thicker. Overlapping more as you wrap also adds thickness. An overgrip adds weight at the handle, so the racket feels a little more head-light: see racket weight and balance. How to fit one, step by step: how to hold a padel racket.

Tacky or dry overgrip?

Tacky

Sticky to the touch. Suits most players and dry or cool conditions, and lasts well when your hands stay dry.

Dry (absorbent)

Soaks up sweat. Suits players with very sweaty hands and hot, humid courts, but expect to change it more often.

Thin or thick

Thinner keeps more feel of the ball. Thicker adds cushion against vibration and builds up the handle.

Make it last

Dry your hands with a towel or wristband between points, air your bag after play and don't leave the racket in a damp place. See racket care.

Stock up on overgrips

If you change one every week, a multi-pack costs less per grip than buying singles. Pick tacky for normal hands, absorbent if you sweat a lot.

Questions players ask

How often should I change my padel overgrip?

About every 8 to 12 hours of play, roughly once a week if you play 2 or 3 times a week. Sooner if it is slippery, damp or torn, and at least every two months even if you hardly play.

How do I know my overgrip needs changing?

It feels smooth or slippery when your hands are dry, the racket turns in your hand, it stays damp, it is torn or coming loose, or it is dark and smells.

How often do pro padel players change their overgrip?

Many fit a new one before every match, and on hot days some change it during the match.

Should I put an overgrip on a new padel racket?

Yes, most players do. The base grip lasts longer under an overgrip, and you can pick the tack and thickness you like.

Can I put two overgrips on a padel racket?

Yes. Two make the handle clearly thicker. Each adds about 8 g at the handle.

How often should I change the base grip?

About every 2 to 3 months if you play several times a week, or once a season if you play now and then, or when it goes flat or hard.
