Padel bags: which bag do you need? A quick bag picker and buying guide.

Racket bag, backpack or just a cover? Answer four questions about how many rackets you carry, how you get to the court and what else you bring. You get the bag type, the size to look for and whether a thermal pocket is worth paying for.

Quick answer

Most club players need a padel backpack (about 20 to 30 litres, 1 or 2 rackets) or a medium racket bag (2 or 3 rackets, about 35 to 45 litres). Pick a bag with a thermal racket pocket if your bag sits in a car or you play in very hot or cold weather, and a separate shoe pocket to keep court sand off your clothes. Large racket bags (up to about 6 rackets) and wheeled bags are for competition players.

Do you need a thermal compartment in a padel bag?

A thermal pocket is a lined racket compartment that slows down heat and cold getting to the racket. Padel rackets have a foam (EVA) core and resin in the face, and shop guides warn that big temperature changes, such as a hot car boot in summer or a freezing one in winter, are bad for them.

Thermal 1

Worth it

Your bag stays in the car between matches, you play outdoors in hot summers or cold winters, or your racket was expensive.

Thermal 2

Nice to have

You go straight from home to the court and back, and the bag spends most of its time indoors.

Thermal 3

What it is not

A thermal pocket protects from temperature, not from knocks. A padded racket pocket and a frame protector handle bumps.

Even with a thermal pocket, take the bag inside after you play. More on looking after your racket: padel racket care.

Padel backpack or racket bag: which is better?

Option 1

Backpack

Light, hands-free and easy on a bike or the train. Fits one racket well. Two rackets plus shoes is a squeeze.

Option 2

Racket bag

Long holdall with a big racket section, so 2 to 6 rackets fit with shoes and clothes. Bulkier to carry, best if you drive.

Option 3

Our view

Start with a backpack. Move to a medium racket bag once you own two rackets or bring a full change of clothes every time.

Questions players ask

What size padel bag do I need?

Most players need a backpack of about 20 to 30 litres for one racket, or a medium racket bag of about 35 to 45 litres for two or three rackets plus shoes and clothes.

How many rackets fit in a padel bag?

A backpack fits one racket well and two only tightly. Medium racket bags hold two or three, and large ones up to about six.

Do I need a thermal padel bag?

It is worth it if your bag stays in a car or you play in very hot or cold weather, because heat and cold can harm the racket's foam core and resin. If you go straight to the court and back, it is a nice extra.

Is a padel backpack or racket bag better?

A backpack is lighter and better for walking, cycling or the train with one racket. A racket bag is better if you carry two or more rackets, a full change of clothes, or you drive.

Can I use a tennis bag for padel?

Yes, if the racket pocket fits a padel racket and protects it. Padel rackets are shorter and thicker than tennis rackets, so check the pocket depth.

Should I leave my padel racket in the car?

Try not to. Hot and cold car boots are bad for rackets. If you must, use a thermal pocket and take it inside as soon as you can.
