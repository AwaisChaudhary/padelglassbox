Padel booking apps, and how to find people to play with.

Most padel courts are booked through an app, and the right one depends on your club and your country. Here is what each main app does, a quick app chooser, and a free invite maker that writes your game message for WhatsApp and splits the court price.

Quick answer

Playtomic is the most widely used padel app for players: you can book courts, join open matches with players at your level and split the payment. In the US many clubs use PlayByPoint, CourtReserve or OpenCourt, often under the club's own app name. In the UK, many LTA registered venues take bookings through ClubSpark, and some use MATCHi or Playtomic. The simple rule: use the app your club uses, which is listed on its website.

How do I find people to play padel with?
The ways that work best, from LTA Padel advice and club organisers.


Start at your club
Ask the front desk about beginner socials, mix-ins and the club WhatsApp group. Staff know who is looking for a game.
Play social formats
Americano and Mexicano nights change partners every round, so you meet 7 or more players in one evening.
Join group coaching
Players in the same group are at a similar level, and the coach can put you together.
Use open matches
Where your club uses an app with open matches, join games at your level. Check the level before you book.
Be honest about your level
Saying you are better than you are gets you fewer invites. Use our level calculator first.
Build your own group
A chat of 6 to 8 people is enough for one game a week; 10 to 15 for flexible games. People drop out, so add more than four.
Same time, same place
Play the same slot each week. The same faces see you and invites follow.
Leagues and ladders
A league gives a season of fixed matches. A ladder suits players without a fixed partner.


Before you join a game, check your level with the level calculator and read the padel etiquette basics: arrive on time, pay your share quickly and call your own lines fairly. Running a club night? Use the Americano and Mexicano generator.


How do I book a padel court for the first time?


1
Find the venue's app
Open the club's website and use the "book" link: it takes you to the app or booking page it uses. Make a free account.


2
Pick a slot and length
Courts are booked by time, often 60 or 90 minutes, for 4 players. Evenings and weekends fill first, so book a few days ahead. See typical prices in how much padel costs.


3
Pay and share
Pay the whole court or split it in the app if it allows. Then send the invite above to your group. Check the cancel rules: many clubs charge if you cancel late.

Questions players ask
What is the best app to book padel courts?
Use the app your club uses. Playtomic is the most widely used padel app for players and also finds open matches. In the US many clubs use PlayByPoint, CourtReserve or OpenCourt, and in the UK many LTA venues use ClubSpark.
How do I find people to play padel with?
Ask your club about socials and its WhatsApp group, play Americano nights, join group coaching, use open matches in your club's app, and build a group chat of 6 to 8 players for a weekly game.
How many people do you need for padel?
Four for a normal doubles game. Book the court for 4 players and split the price four ways.
How do you split the cost of a padel court?
Divide the court price by 4. A £40 court is £10 each. Some apps split the payment for you when you book.
Can I play padel on my own?
Yes. Join an open match in your club's app, a beginner social or an Americano night: you are paired with other players.
Do I need an app to play padel?
Not always. Some clubs take bookings by phone or on their website, but most use an app or online booking page. Check the club's website for its booking link.
