Quick answer

In padel King of the Court, courts are ranked from court 1 (the king court) down. After each short round, the winning pair moves up one court and the losing pair moves down one; winners on court 1 stay, and losers on the bottom court stay. In most versions you change partner every round. The king is the player who spent the most rounds on court 1, or who won court 1 in the last round.

How does King of the Court work in padel?

Courts are ranked

Court 1 is the king court. The other courts go down in order: court 2, court 3 and so on. Everyone wants to get to court 1 and stay there.

Short rounds

Each round is short, often a timed round of about 10 to 15 minutes, or a quick game to a set number of points. Everyone starts and stops at the same time.

Winners up, losers down

The winning pair on each court moves up one court. The losing pair moves down one. Winners on court 1 stay, and losers on the bottom court stay.

New partners

In the usual version, the two pairs that arrive on a court split up, so you play with someone new each round. With fixed pairs, the pair moves together.

Who is king?

Groups use different rules: most rounds played on court 1, the winners of court 1 in the final round, or simply most wins. Pick one before you start.

A draw when time runs out?

Agree a tie rule first. A simple one is to play one more deciding point, like a golden point.

Questions players ask

What is King of the Court in padel?

A social format on several courts. Court 1 is the king court. After each short round, the winners move up one court and the losers move down one, so the best players end up fighting for court 1.

How many players do you need for King of the Court?

At least eight players on two courts. You need four players per court, so 8, 12, 16 or 20 players fit best.

Do you change partners in King of the Court?

In the usual version, yes: the pairs that arrive on each court split up, so you play with someone new each round. Some groups play with fixed pairs instead, and this generator supports both.

How long is a King of the Court round?

Rounds are short, often 10 to 15 minutes, or a quick game to a set number of points. Pick one length and start and stop every court at the same time.

Who wins King of the Court?

It depends on the rule you agree: the player with the most rounds on court 1, the pair that wins court 1 in the last round, or the most wins overall. The generator tracks all three.

What happens to the winners on court 1?

They stay on court 1. Losers on the bottom court also stay where they are. Everyone else moves one court up or down.
