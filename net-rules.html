Padel net rules: can you touch the net?

Racket on the net, a ball that spins back, a swing over the top, a net cord on the serve: tap what happened and get the call with the FIP rule number.

Quick answer

No. If you, your clothes or your racket touch the net, the net posts or the other team's court while the ball is in play, you lose the point. You also lose it if you hit the ball before it has crossed to your side. Your swing may go over the net after you hit the ball on your side, and a rally ball that clips the net and lands over is good. On the serve, a net cord that lands in the box is a let.

What are the net rules in padel?

Lose the point

No touching

While the ball is in play, you may not touch the net, the posts, the net cable or the other team's court. This covers your racket, your body, your clothes and anything you carry.

Lose the point

Wait for the ball

You may only hit the ball once it has crossed the net to your side. No volleying it over their court.

Allowed

Follow-through

If you hit the ball on your side, your racket may go over the net as you finish the swing, as long as it touches nothing.

Allowed

Net cord in a rally

A rally shot that touches the net or a post and then lands in their court is good.

Serve again

Net cord on the serve

A serve that touches the net and lands in the right box is a let. Touching the net and landing anywhere else is a fault. More on the serve rules page.

Allowed

Ball coming back

A ball that bounces on your side and then spins back over the net can still be hit by you, reaching over, as long as you don't touch the net or their court.

Can you reach over the net in padel?

Before the ball crosses: no

If the ball has not yet come over to your side, reaching over to hit it loses the point.

To finish your swing: yes

Hit the ball on your side and let the racket carry on over. Keep your racket and body clear of the net.

When the ball spins back: yes

After a drop shot or a smash with backspin, the ball can bounce and come back to the hitter's side. The other team may reach over or run around to play it, without touching the net or the court.

Some guides add extra conditions, such as only reaching over after the ball has bounced on your side. The FIP rules don't say this: they only say the ball must have crossed the net and you must not touch the net or the other court. In club play, agree how you will call it before you start.

How to stay safe at the net

Stand a step back

About a racket length or two from the net gives you time to react and keeps your racket clear of the net on fast volleys.

Racket up, in front

Keep the racket head up and in front of your body. Balls aimed at you are easier to block, and you are less likely to be hit.

Stop before the net

On a short ball, slow down early. Most net touches happen when a player runs in and can't stop.

Questions players ask

Can you touch the net in padel?

No. If you, your clothes or your racket touch the net, the posts or the net cable while the ball is in play, your team loses the point.

Can your racket go over the net in padel?

Yes, if you hit the ball on your side and the racket carries over as you finish your swing, and you don't touch the net. Hitting the ball before it crosses to your side loses the point.

What happens if the ball spins back over the net in padel?

If it bounces on one side and then comes back over the net, the team on that side may still hit it, even by reaching over the net, as long as they don't touch the net or the other court.

Is a net cord a let in padel?

Only on the serve, when the ball touches the net and lands in the correct service box. In a rally, a ball that clips the net and lands in the other court is in play.

Can you volley the ball before it crosses the net in padel?

No. You must wait until the ball is on your side of the net. Hitting it over the other court loses the point.

Is it a fault to touch the net after the point is over?

The FIP net rule applies while the ball is in play, so a touch after the point has ended does not change the result. Make sure the point is really over first.

What if the ball hits you at the net before bouncing?

Your team loses the point, even if you think the ball was going out.
