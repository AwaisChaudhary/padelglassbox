Padel rules, tap by tap.

Tap any part of the court to see the rule that goes with it, then read the key rules for serving, walls and scoring below.

Quick answer

Padel is played in pairs on a 20 x 10 m court with glass walls. Serve underhand, after one bounce, at or below waist height, diagonally into the box across the net (two tries). The ball must bounce on the ground before it touches a wall on the other side; after that bounce it may hit the glass and stay in play. Scoring is like tennis: 15, 30, 40, game, sets to six, tie-break at 6-6.

What are the basic rules of padel?

The serve

Stand behind the service line with your feet off the lines, bounce the ball, and hit it underhand at or below waist height with one foot on the ground. It must land in the box diagonally opposite. You get two attempts per point.

A serve is a fault if

it lands outside the diagonal box, it's hit above the waist, you miss the ball, your foot touches the line, or after bouncing in the box it hits the metal mesh. Not sure? Use our serve checker.

Lets

If the serve touches the net and still lands in the right box, without touching the metal fence, it is a let and you replay that serve.

Receiving

The receiver must let the serve bounce before hitting it. After that, players can volley freely.

Using the walls

On your side, the ball can bounce once and then hit the glass or fence; you can still play it. You may also hit the ball into your own glass to get it back over the net, but not into your own metal fence. Not sure about a call? Try the wall and fence checker.

Lost points

You lose the point if the ball bounces twice on your side, if your shot hits the opponents' wall or fence before their ground, if it hits your own fence, if the ball touches you or your partner, or if you touch the net or posts during the rally. All the net calls: net rules checker.

Scoring

15, 30, 40, game, sets to six with a tie-break at 6-6 (first to 7, 2 clear): see who serves in a tie-break. At deuce, the organiser chooses advantage, golden point or Star Point: see how each one works.

Court size

The court is 20 m by 10 m, with the service lines 6.95 m from the net. See every measurement, in metres or feet, on our measured court diagram.

Where to stand

Rules tell you what is allowed; position wins points. See where all four players stand at the serve, at the net and at the back on our positioning guide.

Social formats

At club nights you often play points, not sets: an Americano or Mexicano. Our draw maker sets the partners, courts and leaderboard for you.

Changing ends

Change ends after games 1, 3, 5 and every odd game of a set. In a tie-break, change after every 6 points.

Questions players ask

Can you serve overarm in padel?

No. The padel serve is always underhand: you bounce the ball and hit it at or below waist height.

Can the ball hit the wall in padel?

Yes, after it bounces on the ground on your side. If your shot hits the opponents' wall before bouncing on their ground, you lose the point.

What happens if the ball hits a player?

The player's team usually loses the point, with only a few exceptions in the official rules.

How high is a padel net?

88 cm in the middle and 92 cm at the posts.

Where must the server stand?

Behind the service line, which is 6.95 m from the net, between the centre line and the side wall, serving diagonally into the opposite service box.

What is a let in padel?

A serve that touches the net and then lands in the correct box without touching the metal fence. The serve is replayed. A let on the second serve replays only the second serve.

Can you play the ball off your own fence in padel?

No. You may hit the ball into your own glass to send it back over the net, but if your shot hits the metal fence on your side you lose the point.

What happens at 6-6 in padel?

A tie-break: first to 7 points with a 2-point lead. One player serves the first point, then each player serves two points in turn, and you change ends every 6 points. The set ends 7-6.

When do you change ends in padel?

After the 1st, 3rd and every odd game of a set, and after every 6 points in a tie-break.

Is a net cord in play during a padel rally?

Yes. In a rally a ball that touches the net and lands on the other side is in play. Only on the serve does a net touch mean a let.
