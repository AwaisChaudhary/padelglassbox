History of padel: who invented it, and how it grew.

Padel began on one home court in Mexico in 1969 and is now played in more than 150 countries. Here is the full story as a timeline you can filter, from Acapulco to Marbella and Argentina, the pro tours and the UK boom.

Quick answer

Padel was invented in 1969 by Enrique Corcuera in Acapulco, Mexico, who built a court with walls and a net at his home. Prince Alfonso de Hohenlohe took it to Marbella, Spain, in the early 1970s, and it spread to Argentina soon after. The International Padel Federation (FIP) was founded in 1991 and the first World Championships were played in 1992. Premier Padel, today's official pro tour, started in 2022.

Last updated 2026-11-26

Padel history timeline

Show

  1. 1969 · LTA, FIP

    Enrique Corcuera builds the first padel court at his home in Acapulco, Mexico: a 20 x 10 m space with walls and a net. His wife Viviana writes the first rules.

  2. Early 1970s · LTA, FIP

    Prince Alfonso de Hohenlohe takes the game to Spain and builds the first courts at the Marbella Club on the Costa del Sol (LTA gives 1974).

  3. 1970s · LTA, FIP

    Argentine Julio Menditeguy plays it in Marbella and takes it home to Argentina, where it spreads fast (LTA gives 1975).

  4. 1988 · FIP

    The Argentine padel association is founded: the first national association. The first international match, Spain v Argentina, is played in Mar del Plata.

  5. 1989 · FIP

    The first glass court that can be taken apart and moved is introduced in Argentina, the start of today's glass box.

  6. 1991 · LTA, FIP

    The International Padel Federation (FIP) is founded in Madrid on 12 July by the associations of Argentina, Spain and Uruguay.

  7. 1992 · LTA, FIP

    The first World Padel Championships are held in Madrid and Seville. They become a regular event every two years.

  8. 1992 · LTA

    British players form the British Paddle Association so a team can play the 1992 World Championships.

  9. 1993 to 1997 · LTA, FIP

    The spelling 'padel' becomes the standard name. LTA dates it to Spain's sports council in 1993; FIP to a rules agreement between Spain and Argentina in 1997.

  10. 2000 · FIP

    The first World Junior Championship is played in La Plata, Argentina.

  11. 2005 · LTA, FIP

    The first professional tour, the Padel Pro Tour, begins.

  12. 2013 · LTA, FIP

    The World Padel Tour takes over as the main pro tour, until 2023.

  13. 2019 · FIP

    FIP starts its own international circuit, renamed the CUPRA FIP Tour in 2020.

  14. 2019 to 2020 · LTA

    The LTA brings padel into its work in 2019 and becomes the national governing body for padel in Great Britain in 2020.

  15. 2022 · FIP

    Premier Padel starts. It becomes the official world pro tour, run with FIP.

  16. 2024 · FIP

    Premier Padel holds 24 tournaments in 16 countries.

  17. End of 2024 · LTA

    Britain has about 760 courts (51 in 2020) and over 230,000 players (65,000 in 2020).

  18. 2026 · FIP

    FIP counts 100 member federations, padel in more than 150 countries and about 35 million amateur players.

Sources: LTA Padel "History of padel" and the International Padel Federation (FIP) "History" page, both checked 26 Nov 2026. Where they give different years we show both. FIP says the first court had 3 m walls; LTA says walls and fencing of up to 4 m.

Who invented padel?

Enrique Corcuera

In 1969 he built a court with walls and a net at his home in Acapulco, Mexico. LTA says he called it "Paddle Corcuera".

Viviana Corcuera

FIP says his wife wrote the first set of rules. The court size, 20 x 10 m, is still the size used today: see our court dimensions.

Alfonso de Hohenlohe

A Spanish prince who discovered the game in Mexico and built the first courts in Spain at the Marbella Club. That is how padel reached Europe.

Why is padel so big in Spain and Argentina?

Argentina first

Argentina founded the first national association in 1988 and introduced the movable glass court in 1989. LTA says it has more than 10,000 courts.

Then Spain

From the Costa del Sol, padel spread across Spain. LTA says Spain has more than 20,000 courts and about 6 million players.

Now worldwide

FIP says Premier Padel held 24 tournaments in 16 countries in 2024. The 2026 tour's last Major is back where padel began: the Premier Padel Mexico Major in Acapulco. See the world rankings and our padel vs tennis vs pickleball guide.

Why is it called padel?

Early on it was written "paddle", as in "paddle tennis". The Spanish spelling "padel" became the official name in the 1990s, and the world federation uses it. It is not the same game as American paddle tennis or platform tennis: see padel vs racquetball vs platform tennis. Learn the Spanish words players still use in the padel glossary, and the game itself in the padel rules.

Questions players ask

Who invented padel?

Enrique Corcuera, in Acapulco, Mexico, in 1969. He built a court with walls and a net at his home, and his wife Viviana wrote the first rules.

When was padel invented?

In 1969. That makes padel more than 55 years old.

Where does padel come from?

Mexico. It was invented in Acapulco, then grew in Spain (from Marbella, in the 1970s) and in Argentina.

How did padel get to Spain?

Prince Alfonso de Hohenlohe played it in Mexico and built the first Spanish courts at the Marbella Club in the early 1970s.

When did padel come to the UK?

British players formed a padel association in 1992 to play the first World Championships. The LTA became the governing body for padel in Britain in 2020, and the game has grown fast since.

How many people play padel?

FIP says about 35 million amateur players in more than 150 countries (2026). LTA says Britain had over 230,000 players at the end of 2024.
