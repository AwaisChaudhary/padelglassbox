Golden point, Star Point or advantage?

Padel has three ways to finish a game at 40-40. Play the same points through all three below and watch how each one decides the game.

How each format works

Classic

Advantage

At 40-40 (deuce) you need to win two points in a row. Win one and you have the advantage; lose the next and it is deuce again. It can go on for a long time.

Quickest

Golden point

At 40-40 one point decides the game. The receiving pair chooses who receives: the player on the right side or the left. Very common in club games and social padel.

New in 2026

Star Point

At the first deuce you play advantage. If it comes back to 40-40, you play advantage once more. If it comes back to 40-40 a third time, one deciding point, the Star Point, wins the game. The receiving pair chooses the side for that point.

Who uses which

Professional padel

The International Padel Federation (FIP) approved Star Point in late 2025. From 2026 it is used on Premier Padel, the FIP Tour, FIP Promises and the FIP Beyond amateur events.

National events

Some federations follow the pro tours. In the US, USPA-sanctioned tournaments use Star Point from January 2026. Always check the event's own rules.

Your club game

Friendly games can use any of the three. Golden point keeps games short when court time is tight. Agree on one before the first serve, so nobody argues at 40-40.

Tie-breaks

None of this changes the tie-break. At 6-6 you play to 7 points with a two-point lead. See who serves each point on our tie-break page.

Questions players ask

What is the Star Point in padel?

A deuce format used from 2026. You play advantage twice. If the game reaches 40-40 a third time, one deciding point, the Star Point, wins the game.

What is the golden point in padel?

At 40-40 one point decides the game. The receiving pair chooses which player receives it, and whoever wins the point wins the game.

Who chooses the side on the golden point?

The receiving pair. They pick whether the player on the right or the left receives the serve. The same applies to the Star Point.

Is golden point still used in padel?

Yes. Many clubs and social games still use it, as it keeps games short. Professional tours moved to Star Point in 2026.

How many points can a game last with Star Point?

At most 5 points after the first 40-40, so 11 points in total from 0-0. With classic advantage there is no limit.

Which deuce format should we use in a friendly game?

Any of the three is fine. Golden point is quickest, advantage is the most traditional, and Star Point matches the pro game. Just agree before you start.
