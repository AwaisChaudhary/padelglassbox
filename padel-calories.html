Padel calories calculator.

How many calories does your padel game burn? Enter your weight, how long you play and how hard, and get an estimate per session, per hour and per week, plus how much of the weekly activity target it covers.

Quick answer

An hour of padel burns roughly 390 to 630 kcal for a 75 kg (165 lb) adult: about 390 kcal for a relaxed social game, 535 kcal for a normal club match and 630 kcal for a hard, competitive match. Heavier players burn more, lighter players less. These are estimates, not measurements.

Where do these numbers come from?

METs, in one line

A MET compares an activity with sitting still. 1 MET is about 1 kcal per kg of body weight per hour, so a 6 MET activity burns about six times more than resting.

Padel has no official value yet

The 2024 Adult Compendium of Physical Activities, the standard research list of METs, has no padel entry. We use its tennis values as the closest match: doubles for relaxed games, general tennis for club and hard matches.

Why it is only an estimate

Fitness, age, sex, level and how long the rallies last all change the real number. Treat the result as a fair guide, accurate to maybe ± 20 to 30%, not a lab test.

What changes how many calories you burn?

Your body weight

Moving a heavier body costs more energy. At the same effort, a 90 kg player burns about 20% more than a 75 kg player.

Rallies and rests

Padel is stop-start. A 2025 study of national-level players (Frontiers in Sports and Active Living) measured rallies of about 6.5 seconds on average, with about 17 seconds between them. More waiting means fewer calories.

How hard you move

Getting your feet to the ball, moving up and back with your partner and chasing lobs all add up. Standing still and hitting does not.

Your level

Long rallies between good players keep everyone moving. In beginner games the ball is out of play more often, so the burn per hour is lower. See your level with the level calculator.

Warm-up time

A 10-minute warm-up counts too, at a lower rate. The warm-up timer walks you through one.

Watches and apps

A fitness watch uses your heart rate, so its number can be closer for you, or quite different. Wrist sensors can struggle with fast racket movements.

Does padel count as exercise?

The weekly target

The NHS and the World Health Organization advise adults to do at least 150 minutes of moderate activity a week, or 75 minutes of vigorous activity, or a mix of both, plus strength work on 2 days.

Moderate or vigorous?

The US CDC counts 3 to 5.9 METs as moderate and 6 METs or more as vigorous. A relaxed padel game sits in the moderate band; a normal or hard match is usually vigorous, so each minute counts double.

Two games a week

Two 90-minute club matches a week is 180 minutes of vigorous play. That is more than twice the 75-minute vigorous target on its own.

Questions players ask

How many calories does 1 hour of padel burn?

Roughly 390 to 630 kcal for a 75 kg adult: about 390 for a relaxed game, 535 for a normal club match and 630 for a hard match. Lighter players burn less, heavier players more.

How many calories does 90 minutes of padel burn?

A normal 90-minute club match burns about 800 kcal for a 75 kg player, and about 640 kcal for a 60 kg player. A relaxed game burns about a quarter less.

Does padel burn more calories than tennis?

There is no official calorie value for padel yet, so nobody can say for sure. Padel is almost always doubles with short rallies, so it is likely close to tennis doubles for relaxed games and close to general tennis for hard matches.

Is padel good for weight loss?

Padel can help as part of regular activity, because it burns a fair amount of energy and is fun enough to keep doing. Weight loss also depends on diet and sleep; for personal advice, talk to your doctor.

Is padel moderate or vigorous exercise?

A relaxed game is about 5 METs, which is moderate. A normal or hard match is about 7 to 8 METs, which counts as vigorous under the CDC definition (6 METs or more).

How accurate is this padel calorie calculator?

It gives a fair estimate, not a measurement. It uses published METs for tennis because padel has no entry in the Compendium of Physical Activities. Your real number can be 20 to 30% higher or lower.
