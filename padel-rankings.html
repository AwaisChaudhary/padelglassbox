    How does the padel world ranking work?

    Counting

    Best 22 results

    Each player's ranking is the total of their best 22 results in Premier Padel and CUPRA FIP Tour events. Weaker results beyond those 22 do not count.

    Time

    A rolling 52 weeks

    Points stay for one year. When the same week comes round again, last year's points drop off and this year's result replaces them. To keep points, a player must match or beat what they did a year earlier.

    Players

    Points belong to each player

    Padel is played in pairs, but the ranking is for individuals. If a player changes partner, they keep the points they have already won.

    Race

    The Race is this year only

    The FIP Race counts only results from the current calendar year, again the best 22. The top 16 in the Race qualify for the season-ending Premier Padel Finals, held in Barcelona.

    Levels

    Bigger events, more points

    The Premier Padel tour has four levels: Majors, P1, P2 and the Finals. Below it, the CUPRA FIP Tour has Platinum, Gold, Silver and Bronze events, worth far fewer points.

    You

    Not the same as your club level

    The FIP ranking is only for players in pro tour events. Club and app levels, such as Playtomic's, are a separate system. Find yours with the padel level calculator.

    More points for middle rounds

    Titles and finals keep the same points. Quarter-finals, last 16, last 32 and first rounds are worth more, so steady results count for a bit more.

    21 results, not 22

    From 2027 the ranking counts each player's best 21 results instead of 22.

    Top players out of small events

    Higher-ranked players can no longer enter the main draw of lower CUPRA FIP events (Gold, Silver, Bronze), leaving more room for rising players.

    Questions players ask

    Who is the best padel player in the world?

    In the FIP ranking of 23 Nov 2026, Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia share men's number 1 with 20,211 points, and Gemma Triay and Delfina Brea share women's number 1 with 15,831 points.

    How does the padel ranking work?

    The FIP ranking adds up each player's best 22 results in Premier Padel and CUPRA FIP Tour events over a rolling 52 weeks. Points from a year ago drop off and are replaced by the result from the same week this year.

    How many points is a padel Major worth?

    Winning a Major is worth 2,000 FIP points. The runner-up gets 1,200, semi-finalists 720 and quarter-finalists 360 in 2026.

    How many points do you get for winning a P1 or P2?

    A P1 title is worth 1,000 points and a P2 title 600 points. The runners-up get 600 at a P1 and 360 at a P2.

    What is the FIP Race in padel?

    The Race counts only results from the current year, using each player's best 22. The top 16 in the Race qualify for the season-ending Premier Padel Finals.

    Why do padel partners have the same ranking points?

    Points are won by each player, but partners who play the same events together win the same points, so they often end up level and share a ranking place.

    Is the FIP ranking the same as a Playtomic level?

    No. The FIP ranking is for players in professional tour events. Playtomic and club levels are separate rating systems for everyday players.

    What changes in the padel ranking in 2027?

    From 2027 the ranking counts the best 21 results instead of 22, and quarter-finals and earlier rounds give more points. Titles and finals keep the same points.
