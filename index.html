Padel in three rules

All the rules

Serve underhand, diagonally

Bounce the ball behind the service line, hit it at or below waist height, and land it in the box across from you. You get two tries.

Ground first, then the glass

On your side the ball may bounce once and then hit the walls, and it's still in play. If it hits a wall before the ground on your side, the other team wins the point.

Scored like tennis

15, 30, 40, game, with sets to six. At 40-40 many clubs play a golden point, where one deciding point wins the game.

Questions players ask

What is padel?

Padel is a racket sport played mostly in doubles on an enclosed 20 x 10 m court, smaller than a tennis court. You use a solid, holed racket and can play the ball off the glass walls.

How big is a padel court?

A padel court is 20 m long and 10 m wide. The net is 88 cm high in the middle and 92 cm at the posts.

Is padel easy for beginners?

Yes. The underhand serve, small court and walls make long rallies possible from the first game, which is why it's one of the fastest-growing sports.

Is padel scored like tennis?

Yes: 15, 30, 40 and game, with sets to six. Many matches use a golden point at 40-40, and the 2026 rules also allow a format called Star Point.
