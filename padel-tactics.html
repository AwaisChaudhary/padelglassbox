Padel tactics: what shot should I play?

Ten real match situations, each drawn on a court. Pick your shot and see why it works. Then learn the simple rules club players use to win more points.

Quick answer

The core padel tactics: take the net together, because most points are won there; when both opponents are at the net, lob deep or play a soft ball at their feet; only go for a winner on an easy, high ball; play down the middle when they stand close; pick on the weaker player's backhand; and move as a pair, about 3 m apart. At club level most points end with an error, so keep the ball in play.

How do you win a point in padel?

There are only three ways.

1. The opponents make an unforced error

The most common way at club level. Keep one more ball in play than them. Count yours with the match tracker.

2. You force an error

Play at their feet, into the corners, and change speed and direction so they play under pressure.

3. You hit a winner

Only on easy balls: high, slow and near the net. On hard balls, be patient and reset.

Padel tactics for doubles: 8 simple rules

Take the net

The pair at the net wins most points. Serve and move up, and lob to win the net back.

Consistency before power

Fewer errors usually beats more winners. Medium pace, good placement.

Don't rush forward

Go to the net on a good lob or a short ball, not every time. Rushing tires you and leads to rushed shots.

Find the weak spot

Most players have a weaker backhand. Find who is weaker early and play to them.

Use the middle

When opponents stand close or both at the net, the middle causes confusion.

Move them around

Change direction and depth so they run and play off balance.

Move as a pair

Up and back together, about 3 m apart. Call "mine" or "yours". See which side to play.

Keep it simple

Agree one or two plans before the match, such as "lob the left player" or "serve to the body".

Questions players ask

What is the best tactic in padel?

Take the net with your partner and keep the ball in play. Most points at club level end with an error, and the pair at the net wins most points. Lob deep to win the net back when the opponents have it.

What should I do when both opponents are at the net?

Lob deep over them, or play a soft, low ball at their feet so they have to volley up. Then move forward with your partner.

When should I go for a winner in padel?

Only on an easy ball: high, slow and near the net. On difficult balls, be patient and play a safe shot that resets the point.

Why hit down the middle in padel?

A ball down the middle can make both opponents go for it or both leave it, and the net is lowest in the middle.

Should both padel players be at the net?

Ideally yes, together. Move up and back as a pair about 3 m apart, so no gap opens down the middle.

How do you beat better padel players?

Cut your errors, lob a lot to keep them off the net, play to the weaker player's backhand, and keep the ball low and in the middle. Make them hit one more ball.
