Padel match tracker: count your winners and errors.

Tap a shot each time a point ends with your winner or your error. See which shot loses you the most points, and get the fix and a drill for it. Free, no app, no login.

Quick answer

To find out why you lose padel matches, count how each point ends: your winners, your unforced errors, and the opponents' winners and errors. Log the shot that ended the point (serve, return, volley, bandeja, smash, lob, off the glass). After one or two matches, the shot with the most errors is the one to practise first. In pro padel, the pair that won the set made fewer unforced errors in every context studied.

What should I track in a padel match?

Four ways a point can end. You only need to tap one button per point.

How the point endedExampleButton
Your winnerYour smash bounces out of the court; your volley is too fast to reach+ Winner on that shot
Your unforced errorAn easy volley into the net; a return that hits the back glass on the full− Error on that shot
Opponents' errorThey hit a bandeja into the netTheir error
Opponents' winnerTheir víbora wins the point off the side glassTheir winner

A forced error (you just got a racket on a great shot) counts as their winner, not your error. If in doubt, ask: "Would I make that shot nine times out of ten in practice?" If yes, it was unforced.

How do I use my padel stats to improve?

1. Find the biggest leak

The shot with the most errors costs the most points. Errors usually matter more than winners: most points at club level end with a mistake, not a winner.

2. Practise that one shot

Use the drill the tracker suggests for 2 to 3 sessions. Build them into a 4-week training plan.

3. Track again

Log the next match and compare. If errors on that shot fall, move on to the next leak. Your saved matches show the trend.

Do pros make many unforced errors?

Yes. A 2024 study in Biology of Sport (Conde-Ripoll and colleagues) looked at 2,567 professional sets from the World Padel Tour, 2016 to 2022. The main findings:

  • The pair that won the set made fewer unforced errors than the losing pair, in every context the study looked at.
  • Average unforced errors per set were about 6 for men and 7 to 8 for women (for example 6.1 and 7.5 in first sets).
  • Unforced errors per set fell season by season for both men and women.

So the lesson for club players is simple: cutting errors wins more sets than hitting more winners.

Questions players ask

How do I track my padel stats?

Count how each point ends: your winner, your unforced error, or the opponents' winner or error, and note the shot. This page does it with one tap per point and saves your matches on your phone.

What is an unforced error in padel?

A mistake on a ball you would normally get back, such as an easy volley into the net or a ball that hits the back glass on the full. If you were under real pressure, it is a forced error and counts as the opponents' winner.

Is there a free padel stats app?

This tracker is free and works in your phone's browser with no app or login. Add it to your home screen to open it like an app. Your data stays on your device.

Do winners or errors matter more in padel?

Errors usually matter more. In a study of professional sets, the pair that won made fewer unforced errors in every context. At club level, most points end with a mistake.

How many unforced errors do pro padel players make?

A 2024 study of World Padel Tour sets found about 6 unforced errors per set for men and 7 to 8 for women on average.

Can I track my partner's stats too?

Yes. Switch 'Who hit the last shot?' to 'My partner' before you tap. The summary shows both of you and the pair total.
