Table tennis rules and table size

The official table, net and ball sizes, then the rules of the serve, scoring and doubles, with a quick quiz.

Quick answer

A table tennis table is 2.74 m long, 1.525 m wide and 76 cm high (9 × 5 ft, 2 ft 6 in high), with a net 15.25 cm (6 in) high. Games go to 11, win by 2, and each player serves two points in turn (one each from 10-10). Toss the ball at least 16 cm straight up from an open palm and hit it so it bounces once on your side, then on your opponent's.

The serve

Rest the ball on an open, flat palm behind the end line and above the table. Toss it nearly straight up at least 16 cm and hit it as it falls, so it bounces on your side then your opponent's. The ball must stay visible to the receiver: no hiding it with your body or free arm. If a serve clips the net and lands correctly, it's a let and you serve again.

Scoring

Every rally scores a point. A game goes to 11, win by 2. Serve passes every two points, and every point from 10-10. Matches are best of three, five or seven games, and players change ends after each game (and at 5 points in the deciding game).

Losing a point

You lose the point if you miss the ball, hit it into the net or off the table, let it bounce twice on your side, volley it before it bounces, move the table, touch the net or put your free hand on the table during a rally. A ball that touches the edge of the table is good; one off the side of the table is not.

Doubles

The serve goes from the server's right half to the receiver's right half. Partners must hit in turn. Serve rotates so every player serves to and receives from each opponent.

Questions players ask

What size is a table tennis table?

2.74 m long, 1.525 m wide and 76 cm high (9 × 5 ft, 2 ft 6 in high).

How high is a table tennis net?

15.25 cm (6 in).

How many points is a game of table tennis?

11, win by 2. Serve changes every two points, and every point from 10-10.

How high do you have to toss the ball to serve?

At least 16 cm, nearly straight up, from an open palm.

How much room do I need for a table tennis table?

About 6 × 3 m for casual play; competition areas are 14 × 7 m.
