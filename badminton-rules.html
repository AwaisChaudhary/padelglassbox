Badminton rules, explained simply

Scoring, the serve (including the 1.15 m rule), faults and lets, for singles and doubles, with a quick quiz.

Quick answer

A match is the best of three games. Each game is to 21 points with rally scoring (every rally wins a point); at 20-20 you need a 2-point lead, and at 29-29 the next point wins. Serve diagonally, hitting the shuttle below 1.15 m from the floor, from the right court when your score is even and the left when it's odd.

Last updated 2026-11-25

1. Scoring

Rally scoring: whoever wins the rally scores and serves next. Games go to 21, win by 2, capped at 30 (the side reaching 30 at 29-all wins). Best of three games. There's a 60-second break when the leader reaches 11, and up to 2 minutes between games. Players change ends after each game, and in the third game when the leader reaches 11.

2. The serve

Serve diagonally into the opposite service court. When the racket hits it, the whole shuttle must be below 1.15 m from the court surface, and the first contact must be on the base (cork). Both feet stay on the floor and inside the service court until the serve is made, without touching the lines.

3. Which side to serve from

Serve from the right service court when the server's score is even (0, 2, 4…) and the left when it's odd. In doubles, only the side that wins a rally changes service courts; there is one serve per side, and the receiving pair don't swap places.

4. Faults

A rally is lost if the shuttle lands outside the lines, goes through or under the net, hits the ceiling, walls or a person, is hit twice in a row by the same player (or by both partners in a row), or if a player touches the net or reaches over it to hit the shuttle. A shuttle landing on a line is in.

5. Lets

A let replays the rally. Examples: the receiver wasn't ready when the serve was hit, the shuttle gets caught on the net after the serve, or there's outside interference such as a shuttle from another court.

6. Singles vs doubles lines

Singles serves go long and narrow (to the back line, singles sidelines); doubles serves go short and wide (to the doubles long service line, outer sidelines). See the badminton court dimensions.

Based on the BWF Laws of Badminton. Club and school games often play shorter formats, such as a single game to 21 or to 15.

Questions players ask

How many points is a badminton game?

21, with rally scoring. At 20-20 you need a 2-point lead; at 29-29 the next point wins.

What is the 1.15 m serve rule in badminton?

When the server hits the shuttle, the whole shuttle must be below 1.15 m from the court surface.

How many games in a badminton match?

Best of three games.

Can you touch the net in badminton?

No. Touching the net with your racket, body or clothing while the shuttle is in play is a fault.

Is the line in or out in badminton?

In. A shuttle landing on a line counts as in.
