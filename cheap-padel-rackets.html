Cheap padel rackets that are actually good.

You don't need a $300 racket to start. Here is what a good budget racket looks like, eight that reviewers rate under $100 / £100 / €100, and what you give up by spending less.

Quick answer

Yes, a cheap padel racket is fine for beginners. Look for a round shape, 355 to 370 g, a soft EVA core and a low (head-light) balance, which costs about £55 to £115. Good picks under €100 include the Adidas Match 2026, Siux Beat Hybrid 2026, Head Bolt 2026 and Babolat Air Origin.

Last updated 2026-11-25

What should a cheap padel racket have?

ShapeRound. The sweet spot sits low and in the middle, so mis-hits still go over.
Weight355 to 370 g. Lighter is kinder to your elbow while your technique is new.
CoreSoft EVA or foam. More comfort, and the ball comes off the face easily.
BalanceLow (head-light). Quicker at the net and easier to control.
FaceFibreglass, or fibreglass with some carbon. Softer and more forgiving than full carbon.

What do you give up with a cheap racket?

  • Less power and spin on smashes and bandejas than carbon rackets. Beginners rarely notice.
  • A shorter life. Soft budget cores lose their pop sooner if you play several times a week.
  • Fewer weight options. Most come in one weight.

When you play twice a week and hit the ball cleanly, that is the time to move up. See our weight and balance guide first.

Questions players ask

Is a cheap padel racket good enough for a beginner?

Yes. A round, 355 to 370 g racket with a soft EVA core and a low balance forgives mis-hits and is easy on the arm. Most beginners don't benefit from a full-carbon racket.

How much should I spend on my first padel racket?

About £55 to £115 (or $60 to $120) buys a good first racket. Put the rest toward court time and a lesson, which will improve your game faster than a more expensive racket.

What is the best padel racket under $100?

Reviewers' budget favourites include the Adidas Match 2026, Siux Beat Hybrid 2026, Head Bolt 2026, Dunlop Megamax 2026 and Babolat Air Origin. Pick a round one if you are new.

Are cheap padel rackets bad for your elbow?

Not if you choose a light racket with a soft core. Heavy, stiff, head-heavy rackets are the ones that strain the elbow.
