Squash court dimensions

The floor, the wall lines and the tin of a regulation singles squash court, in metres and feet.

Quick answer

A singles squash court is 9.75 m long and 6.40 m wide (32 × 21 ft). On the front wall, the out line is 4.57 m high, the service line 1.78 m and the top of the tin 0.48 m. The back wall out line is 2.13 m high. The short line is 5.44 m from the front wall and the service boxes are 1.60 m square.

The side wall out lines slope from the front wall line (4.57 m) down to the back wall line (2.13 m). Doubles courts are wider: 9.75 × 7.62 m. Coming from squash? See padel vs squash.

Questions players ask

What are the dimensions of a squash court?

9.75 m long and 6.40 m wide (32 × 21 ft) for singles.

How high is the tin in squash?

The top of the tin is 0.48 m (19 in) from the floor on a standard court.

How high is the service line in squash?

1.78 m (5 ft 10 in) on the front wall.

How far is the short line from the front wall?

5.44 m (17 ft 10 in).

How big is a squash service box?

1.60 m square (about 5 ft 3 in), measured to the outside of the lines.
