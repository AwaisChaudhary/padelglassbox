Are your padel balls dead?

Six quick checks tell you if a ball is still good for a match, fine for practice, or ready for the bin. Then learn how long padel balls last and how to keep them lively.

Quick answer

For relaxed social games, a can of padel balls (usually 3 balls) stays playable for about 3 to 5 sessions. For a match that matters, open a new can. A ball is dead when it squashes easily, bounces lower than a new ball, sounds dull and dies off the back glass. Padel balls have less pressure than tennis balls (about 11 psi vs 14 psi), so don't swap one for the other.

How long do padel balls last?

Social play: a few sessions

For relaxed games, a can usually stays playable for about three to five sessions. After that the bounce gets lower and rallies off the glass get harder.

Serious matches: open a new can

Balls lose their best bounce quickly when you hit hard. For a match that matters, most players open a fresh can and keep the older balls for practice.

Pressure leaks from day one

Padel balls are filled with air under pressure. Once the can is open, the pressure slowly leaks out, even if you don't play.

Courts wear the felt

Sand on artificial grass courts rubs the felt and makes it heavy. Wet or dirty balls slow down a lot.

Heat and cold change the feel

On a cold day even new balls feel flat. Keep opened cans away from hot cars and cold garages; a cool, dry cupboard is best.

Pressurisers help a little

A ball saver tube keeps air in for longer, but it can't fix worn felt or bring a dead ball back to life.

How do I make padel balls last longer?

Close the can

Put the lid back on tightly after every game, or use a ball saver.

Keep them dry

Don't play with wet balls if you can avoid it, and let them dry at room temperature, not on a heater.

Mark your cans

Write the date or a tally on the lid each time you play. Then you know which can is fresh and which is for drills.

Use old balls well

Old balls are fine for warming up, drills and practice serves. Just not for your next match. Check if a serve is legal while you practise.

Questions players ask

How long do padel balls last?

For social games, a can usually lasts about three to five sessions. Players who hit hard or play important matches often open a new can each match, because the best bounce goes after one or two uses.

How do I know if a padel ball is dead?

Squeeze it, drop it next to a new ball and watch it off the back glass. A dead ball feels soft, bounces lower, sounds dull and dies in the corner instead of coming back.

Can you use tennis balls for padel?

It's better not to. Tennis balls have more pressure inside, so they bounce higher and faster. Padel balls are made to play off the glass at the right speed.

What is the pressure of a padel ball?

LTA Padel gives about 11 psi for a new padel ball, compared with about 14 psi for a new tennis ball.

Are padel balls smaller than tennis balls?

Only very slightly. A padel ball is 6.35 to 6.77 cm across and a tennis ball 6.54 to 6.86 cm, and both weigh 56.0 to 59.4 g.

Do ball pressurisers work?

They can slow down the loss of pressure, but they can't repair worn felt or make a dead ball lively again.

How many balls come in a can of padel balls?

Most cans or tubes hold 3 padel balls. Shops also sell multi-packs of several cans, which work out cheaper if you play every week.

Can I play padel with old balls?

Yes, for warm-ups, drills and relaxed practice. For a match, open a fresh can: old balls bounce low and die off the glass, which changes how the game plays.
