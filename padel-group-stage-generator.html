Padel group stage generator: groups, then a knockout.

The format most club tournaments use. Type your pairs, pick the group size, and get balanced groups with every match listed. Type the games won, and the tables sort themselves. When the groups are done, send the qualifiers to our knockout bracket in one tap. Free, no app, saved on this device.

Quick answer

Split the pairs into groups of 3 or 4, play a round robin in each group (3 matches in a group of 3, 6 in a group of 4), rank by matches won, then game difference, then games won, and send the top 1 or 2 of each group into a knockout. 12 pairs in 3 groups of 4 = 18 group matches, then 6 qualifiers. 16 pairs in 4 groups of 4 = 24 group matches, then a quarter-final knockout with the top 2.

How many matches in a padel group stage?

In a round robin group every pair plays every other pair once: a group of n pairs has n x (n - 1) / 2 matches.

How are padel group tables ranked?

1st

Matches won

Each win counts once. This tool does not give points for a loss, so a pair that wins more matches is always higher.

2nd

Game difference

Games won minus games lost across all group matches. It rewards clear wins and close losses.

3rd

Games won, then head to head

If still level, more games won goes higher. Many organisers then use the result between the two tied pairs. If three pairs are tied, agree a rule before the event starts and tell the players.

These are common club rules, not an official FIP rule: every tournament sets its own tie-breaks in its rules. Check yours before you start, and write them on the WhatsApp group.

Groups of 3 or groups of 4?

Groups of 3

Each pair plays 2 group matches. Quick, and good when court time is short. One bad match can knock a pair out, and the same pair sits out every third match.

Groups of 4

Each pair plays 3 matches and two matches can be played at the same time, which suits 2 courts per group. The most common choice for club events.

Groups of 5

Each pair plays 4 matches: great value for a full day, but 10 matches per group. Use short formats like a race to 4 games.

Questions players ask

How do you run a padel tournament with groups and a knockout?

Split the pairs into groups of 3 or 4, spread the strongest pairs across the groups, play every match in each group, then send the top 1 or 2 of each group into a knockout bracket.

How many matches are in a padel group of 4?

6 matches. Each pair plays the other 3 pairs once. A group of 3 has 3 matches and a group of 5 has 10.

How are ties broken in a padel group?

Most clubs use matches won, then game difference, then games won, then the result between the tied pairs. There is no single official rule, so set yours before the event.

How long does a padel group stage take?

Count the group matches and divide by your courts. 12 pairs in 3 groups of 4 is 18 matches: on 3 courts that is 6 rounds of about 35 to 45 minutes with one-set matches.

How many pairs should go through from each group?

Take enough to fill a knockout of 4, 8 or 16 if you can. With 4 groups, the top 2 make a quarter-final. With 3 groups, the top 2 make 6 qualifiers and the 2 best group winners get a bye.
