Padel court dimensions, measured.

Every official measurement on one court you can explore: tap a measurement to light it up and read what it's for. Switch between metres and feet.

Quick answer

A doubles padel court is 20 m long and 10 m wide (about 65 ft 7 in by 32 ft 10 in). The service line is 6.95 m from the net, the net is 0.88 m high in the middle and 0.92 m at the posts, and the back wall is 4 m high: 3 m of glass plus 1 m of mesh. Indoor courts need at least 6 m of clear height.

Small building tolerances are allowed. Some courts use a variant with taller side glass, and rules for new venues can ask for more height. If you are building a court, check the current rules with your national federation.

What the measurements mean for your game

Smaller than a tennis court

At 20 m by 10 m, the court is about three quarters the area of a tennis doubles court (23.77 m by 10.97 m), and there is no run-off space behind the walls. You rarely sprint far, which is a big reason padel feels easy to start. See all three sports to scale on our padel vs tennis vs pickleball page, or check how many courts fit in your space with the court space planner.

A short back area

Only about 3 m sits behind the service line. Balls reach the back glass quickly, so learning to let the ball come off the wall matters early.

A low net

The net is lower than in tennis, and dips a little in the middle. Low, flat balls over the centre are the safest way to keep the ball in play.

Where the glass steps down

The side glass is tallest near the back walls. Balls that hit the mesh act differently from glass, which you can read about on our wall and fence rules page.

Questions players ask

How big is a padel court?

A doubles padel court is 20 m long and 10 m wide inside the walls. That is about 65 ft 7 in by 32 ft 10 in.

How far is the service line from the net?

6.95 m on each side. That leaves 3.05 m between the service line and the back wall.

How high is a padel net?

0.88 m in the middle and 0.92 m at the posts, which is roughly 2 ft 11 in and 3 ft.

How high are the walls of a padel court?

The back wall is 4 m in total: 3 m of glass with 1 m of metal mesh on top. The side glass is 3 m high near the back walls, then steps down to 2 m.

How much ceiling height does indoor padel need?

The official rules ask for at least 6 m of clear height above the court. Higher is better for lobs.

How big is a singles padel court?

Singles courts are 20 m long like doubles, but only 6 m wide.

Is a padel court smaller than a tennis court?

Yes. A padel court is 20 x 10 m (200 square metres). A tennis doubles court is 23.77 x 10.97 m (about 261 square metres), so padel is about three quarters of the area, before counting the space tennis needs around the lines.
