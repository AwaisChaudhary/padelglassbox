Padel score keeper.

Put your phone by the net and tap the side that wins each point. It handles deuce, golden point, tie-breaks and sets, and remembers the score if you close the page.

How padel scoring works

Points

0, 15, 30, 40, then the game. Exactly like tennis.

Deuce

At 40-40 there are three official options: classic advantage, golden point (one deciding point) or the newer Star Point. All three are built in here. Not sure which to pick? See golden point vs Star Point.

Sets and tie-breaks

A set goes to six games with a two-game lead. At 6-6 a tie-break is played to 7 points, two clear. Most matches are best of three sets.

Golden point side

With golden point, the receiving pair chooses which player receives the deciding serve.

Star Point

You play advantage twice. If the game comes back to 40-40 a third time, one deciding point wins it. The score keeper counts the deuces for you.

Questions players ask

How do you keep score in padel?

Like tennis: 0, 15, 30, 40 and game. Sets go to six games with a two-game lead, and a tie-break decides 6-6.

What is the golden point in padel?

At 40-40, one deciding point is played. The receiving pair chooses which side receives, and whoever wins that point wins the game.

What is Star Point in padel?

Star Point is a deuce format used on the pro tours from 2026. You play advantage twice; if the game reaches 40-40 a third time, one deciding point wins it. This score keeper supports it.

Does this score keeper save my match?

Yes. The score is saved on your phone, so you can lock the screen or close the page and carry on.
