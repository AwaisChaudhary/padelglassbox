Court sizes compared

Padel, tennis, pickleball, badminton, squash and table tennis drawn to the same scale, plus a planner that tells you how many courts fit in your space.

Quick answer

Smallest to biggest: table tennis table 2.74 × 1.525 m, squash 9.75 × 6.4 m, badminton 13.4 × 6.1 m, pickleball 13.41 × 6.1 m, padel 20 × 10 m, tennis 23.77 × 10.97 m. On one tennis court with its run-off (120 × 60 ft, 36.6 × 18.3 m) you can fit 4 pickleball courts or 4 badminton courts, but only 1 padel court with the LTA's spacing: padel needs 20.8 m of width, so widen the plot by about 2.5 m and 3 padel courts fit side by side.

Why only one padel court on a tennis court?

A padel court is 20 m long, and a standard tennis plot is only 18.3 m wide. Laid the other way, two padel courts end to end need 41.6 m, more than the 36.6 m plot. Three padel courts side by side need 20.8 × 33.8 m, so a plot about 2.5 m wider than usual, or a bank of tennis courts, takes three. Ask an installer: some fit courts closer than the LTA spacing.

How much space does a padel court need?

20 × 10 m inside the glass, and 20.8 × 10.8 m with the LTA's margin around it. Walls are 4 m high at the ends. Indoors, the LTA asks for at least 6 m of clear height at the court ends and 8 m over the net. Costs: see padel court cost.

Pickleball on a tennis court

Four pickleball courts fit on a tennis court with its run-off, each with a 30 × 60 ft area. With only the tennis lines (78 × 36 ft) most clubs paint one or two. Lines and net: pickleball court dimensions.

Questions players ask

How many padel courts fit on a tennis court?

One, on a standard tennis plot with run-off (36.6 × 18.3 m), using the LTA's spacing. Padel needs 20.8 m of width, so a plot widened by about 2.5 m (20.8 × 36.6 m) takes three courts side by side.

How many pickleball courts fit on a tennis court?

Four, using the whole tennis area with run-off (120 × 60 ft), with 30 × 60 ft for each pickleball court.

Is a padel court bigger than a badminton court?

Yes. Padel is 20 × 10 m (200 m²); a badminton doubles court is 13.4 × 6.1 m (about 82 m²), so padel is about 2.4 times bigger.

Which is bigger, a padel court or a tennis court?

A tennis doubles court: 23.77 × 10.97 m (about 261 m²) against padel's 20 × 10 m (200 m²). Tennis also needs much more run-off.

How much space do you need for a padel court?

20.8 × 10.8 m for one court with the LTA's margin, plus 1.5 m between courts. Three courts side by side need 20.8 × 33.8 m.

Are pickleball and badminton courts the same size?

Almost: both are about 44 × 20 ft. The kitchen line and the net height differ.
