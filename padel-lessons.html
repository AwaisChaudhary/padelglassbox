Padel lessons: what they cost and what to expect.

Private, shared or group: see typical padel lesson prices in the UK and US, work out what your lesson really costs each, and learn what happens in a first lesson and how to pick a good coach.

Quick answer

A private padel lesson usually costs about £40 to £80 an hour in the UK and about $50 to $160 an hour in the US. Group sessions and clinics are cheaper: about £10 to £30 or $20 to $60 per person. Many US clubs run 60-minute intro clinics for free to $40. Sharing a private lesson with 1 to 3 friends cuts the cost each a lot.

Private, shared or group: which padel lesson should you book?

Private

Best for fixing one thing fast

The coach watches only you. Good for a stubborn problem like your grip, serve or play off the back glass. It is the most expensive option per person.

Shared private

Best value with a regular partner

Two to four friends split one coach. Many clubs charge only a small extra amount per added player, so the cost each drops a lot. You also learn to play as a pair.

Group clinic

Best for your first steps

A cheap way to learn the basics and meet players at your level. You get less personal feedback, and you may be grouped with people at a different level.

Course

Best for building a habit

A weekly block of 4 to 6 sessions with the same coach and group. You repeat skills often enough to keep them.

What happens in a first padel lesson?

Most coaches cover the same basics. The order and timing below are a typical example for a 60-minute session.

AboutWhat you do
5 to 10 minWarm-up and a short chat about your sport background. Some clubs do a quick level check.
10 minHow to hold the racket (the continental grip) and the ready position. Strap round your wrist.
10 to 15 minForehand and backhand from the back of the court, with control rather than power.
10 minLetting the ball come off the back glass and hitting it after it rebounds. This is the new skill for most people.
10 minVolleys at the net and where to stand with your partner.
5 minThe underarm serve: bounce, hit at or below the waist, land it diagonally.
5 to 10 minA short game or cooperative rally to put it together. Basic scoring.

Most coaches leave the smash, bandeja and víbora for later lessons. Read ahead with our guides to the padel grip, playing off the walls and the serve.

What should I bring to my first padel lesson?

Racket and balls

Intro sessions and clinics usually provide them. Ask when you book. If you buy later, a round racket is easiest to learn with: see the racket finder.

Shoes

Padel or tennis shoes grip best on sandy turf. Trainers are usually fine for a first intro session, but not for regular play. See the padel shoes guide.

Clothes and water

Normal gym clothes, layers if you play outdoors in winter, and a full water bottle. Arrive about 10 minutes early. Full list in the kit planner.

How do I find a good padel coach?

In the UK

Use the LTA "Find a coach" tool with the padel filter, or ask your club which coach runs beginner sessions. The main LTA qualification is the Level 2 Padel Instructor, a five-day course. Check the coach is insured and has safeguarding and first-aid training, especially for children.

In the US and elsewhere

Ask the club's pro shop or front desk, or look in the coaching section of booking apps such as Playtomic. There is no single required padel qualification everywhere, so ask what padel-specific training and experience the coach has. Some tennis pros also coach padel.

Good signs

The coach asks about your level and goals first, the lesson has a clear plan, they give you one or two things to work on, and they tell you what to do next. A "lesson" that is only feeding balls with no feedback is poor value.

How many padel lessons does a beginner need?

There is no fixed number. A common path, in our own words:

  • Take one intro session or lesson before your first proper match, so you know the grip, the serve and how the walls work.
  • Add two or three more hours of coached play or clinics, then join beginner open play or a social like an Americano.
  • After that, many players book a 4 to 6 week course or a block of shared lessons, and take a private lesson when one thing keeps going wrong.

Questions players ask

How much does a padel lesson cost?

A private padel lesson usually costs about £40 to £80 an hour in the UK and about $50 to $160 an hour in the US. Group sessions and clinics cost about £10 to £30 or $20 to $60 per person.

How much is a group padel lesson?

In the UK about £10 to £30 per person per session. In the US clinics usually cost about $20 to $60 per person, and many clubs run 60-minute intro clinics for free to $40.

Can I share a private padel lesson with friends?

Usually yes. Many coaches let 2 to 4 players share one private lesson for a small extra fee per player, which makes the cost each much lower. Ask when you book.

How long is a padel lesson?

Most private lessons are 60 minutes. Group clinics are usually 60 or 90 minutes, and US intro clinics are usually 60 minutes.

What do you learn in a first padel lesson?

The grip and ready position, forehand and backhand, playing the ball after it comes off the back glass, volleys at the net, the underarm serve and a short game with basic scoring.

Do I need my own racket for a padel lesson?

Not for a first lesson. Intro sessions and clinics usually lend rackets and balls. Ask the club when you book.

How many padel lessons do I need as a beginner?

There is no fixed number. One intro session before your first match, then two or three more hours of coaching or clinics, is a common way to start. After that many players book a 4 to 6 week course.
