Padel tie-break rules, point by point.

Not sure who serves next in a tie-break, or when to change ends? Enter the four players, tap who wins each point, and the tracker shows the next server, the side to serve from and when to swap ends.

Quick answer

At 6-6 padel plays a tie-break: first to 7 points with a 2-point lead wins, and the set ends 7-6. The next server in the set's order serves point 1 from the right. After that each player serves two points in turn (first from the left, then the right), keeping the same order. Change ends after every 6 points. A match tie-break (super tie-break) at one set all is the same, but first to 10.

How does a padel tie-break work?

When it starts

When a set reaches 6-6. The tie-break is the last game of the set, and the pair that wins it wins the set 7-6.

How you count

Forget 15, 30, 40. In a tie-break you count 0, 1, 2, 3 and so on. The first pair to 7 points wins, but you must be 2 points ahead, so 7-6 is not enough: play on to 8-6, 9-7 and so on.

The first serve

The player whose turn it is to serve in the set serves the first point only, from the right side of the court.

Then two serves each

The other pair serves the next two points, then each player serves two points in a row, in the same order as the rest of the set. The first of each two is served from the left, the second from the right.

Change ends every 6 points

Swap ends after 6, 12, 18 points and so on. There is no long rest: play is meant to be continuous, and the FIP rules give 20 seconds for the change.

The next set

The next set starts with a serve from the pair that did not serve first in the tie-break.

Common tie-break mix-ups

"First to 7 wins"

Only with a 2-point lead. At 6-6 in the tie-break, keep going until one pair is 2 points clear.

"Golden point at 6-6 in the tie-break"

Golden point and Star Point are for 40-40 in a normal game. A tie-break is counted in single points and needs 2 clear. How deuce formats work.

Serving from the wrong side

Point 1 is served from the right. After that, each player serves their first point from the left and their second from the right.

Forgetting to swap ends

Use the total of both scores: when it reaches 6, 12 or 18, swap. At 4-2, 5-1 or 3-3 it's time to change.

Questions players ask

How many points is a padel tie-break?

A set tie-break is first to 7 points with a 2-point lead, so it can go past 7, for example 8-6 or 10-8. A match tie-break (super tie-break) is first to 10, also with a 2-point lead.

Who serves first in a padel tie-break?

The player whose turn it is to serve in the set. They serve only the first point, from the right. Then the other pair serves two points, and everyone serves two points in turn.

When do you change ends in a padel tie-break?

After every 6 points: when the total score reaches 6, 12, 18 and so on. The change should be quick; the FIP rules allow 20 seconds.

What is a super tie-break in padel?

A tie-break to 10 points, 2 clear, played at one set all instead of a full third set. It is an option the organiser can choose, often used in club leagues and some events.

Who serves first in the set after a tie-break?

The pair that did not serve first in the tie-break starts the next set.

Is there a golden point in a padel tie-break?

No. Golden point and Star Point are ways to settle 40-40 in a normal game. In a tie-break you need to win by 2 points.

What is the score of a set won on a tie-break?

7-6. The tie-break counts as the deciding game of the set.
