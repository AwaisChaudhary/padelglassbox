Pickleball serve rules (2026): volley serve, drop serve and a legal-serve checker

How to serve in pickleball under the 2026 USA Pickleball rules, what changed this year, and a quick checker to settle "was that serve legal?" on court.

Quick answer

Serve diagonally, with both feet behind the baseline. With a volley serve, three things must be clearly true when you hit the ball: the paddle is moving in an upward arc, the top of the paddle head is not above your wrist, and the ball is no higher than your waist. Or use a drop serve: drop the ball, let it bounce, then hit it. The serve must clear the kitchen (a serve on the kitchen line is a fault) and land in the diagonal service court. You get one serve; a serve that clips the net and lands in is in play.

How do you serve in pickleball, step by step?

Step 1

Call the score

Before serving, say the score: in doubles three numbers (your score, their score, server 1 or 2), in singles two. The first serve of a doubles game is called 0-0-2.

Step 2

Stand in the right place

Both feet behind the baseline, between the centreline and the sideline (imagine them extended). Serve from the right side when your score is even in singles; in doubles you start on the right and switch with your partner each time you win a point on serve.

Step 3

Choose volley or drop

Volley serve: hit the ball out of the air with an upward swing, below the waist. Drop serve: drop the ball from your hand, let it bounce, then hit it.

Step 4

Aim diagonally, deep

The ball must clear the kitchen (the 7 ft non-volley zone) and land in the service court across from you. Deep serves give the receiver less time.

Step 5

Then let it bounce

After the serve, the return must bounce, and your next shot must bounce too (the two-bounce rule). Only then can anyone volley.

What changed in the 2026 pickleball serve rules?

The 2026 USA Pickleball rulebook took effect on 1 January 2026 and was renumbered; the serve is now rule 7.

Topic2026 ruleWhat it means
Upward motion (7.C.1)The paddle must move in a clear upward arc at contactBefore 2026 the rule talked about the arm. Now it is the paddle that counts.
"Clearly"Each volley-serve test must be clearly metIf the referee can't tell, it is a fault. Borderline serves are no longer given the benefit of the doubt.
Spin (7.B.2)No spin added with the hand or body before contactNo finger-spin release. Spin from the paddle at contact is still fine, on both serves.
Landing (7.E)Must clear the non-volley zone, with or without touching the net, and land in the correct courtNet serves that land in are played. Wrong court is a fault.
Scoring (provisional)Rally scoring allowed in some events; the receiving side can win the game on the last rallyTraditional side-out scoring is still standard at most clubs. Check which one your event uses.

Common serve faults

Serve on the kitchen line

The kitchen line is part of the kitchen, so a serve that touches it is a fault. Sidelines, the centreline and the baseline are in.

Foot on the baseline

Stepping on or over the baseline before you hit the ball is a foot fault. Stay behind it until contact.

Paddle above the wrist

On a volley serve, the top of the paddle head must stay below your wrist at contact. A high, tennis-style swing is a fault. Use a drop serve if you like a freer swing.

Tossing on a drop serve

The drop serve is a drop. Throwing the ball up or bouncing it down hard is a fault.

Serving from the wrong side

In singles, serve from the right when your score is even and the left when it is odd. In doubles, check who should be serving before every point.

Questions players ask

What are the serve rules in pickleball?

Serve diagonally with both feet behind the baseline. A volley serve must be hit with the paddle moving upward, the paddle head not above the wrist and the ball below the waist. Or drop the ball and hit it after it bounces. The serve must clear the kitchen, including its line, and land in the diagonal court. You get one serve.

What is the difference between a volley serve and a drop serve?

A volley serve is hit out of the air and must follow three motion rules (upward paddle, paddle head below wrist, ball below waist). A drop serve is dropped from the hand and hit after it bounces, and those motion rules do not apply.

Is a serve that hits the net legal in pickleball?

Yes. There are no service lets. A serve that touches the net and still clears the kitchen and lands in the correct court is in play. If it lands in the kitchen or out, it is a fault.

Can you put spin on a pickleball serve?

Yes, from the paddle. Under the current rules you may not spin the ball with your hand or body when you release it, but spin made by the paddle at contact is allowed on both serves.

Do you get two serves in pickleball?

No. Each server gets one attempt. In doubles both partners serve before the serve passes to the other side, except at the start of the game, when only one player serves.

Is a serve on the kitchen line in?

No. The kitchen line is part of the non-volley zone, so a serve that lands on it is a fault. A serve on the sideline, centreline or baseline is in.
