Padel team generator: fair doubles pairs in one tap

Type in your players and, if you like, a level for each. Get balanced pairs, the match on each court and who sits out. Re-draw as often as you want. Free, no sign-up, names saved on this device.

Quick answer

For fair padel doubles, sort the players by level and pair the strongest with the weakest, the second strongest with the second weakest, and so on. Then put the two pairs with the closest totals on the same court. This generator does that for you, with a small random shuffle so the pairs change each draw. Without levels it makes a fully random draw.

How do you make fair padel teams?

Rule 1

Balance pairs, not players

In padel the unit is the pair. Two strong players together beat everyone; one strong and one weaker player make a fair team.

Rule 2

Strongest with weakest

Sort by level and pair 1st with last, 2nd with second last. Each pair ends up with a similar total. This is what the generator does.

Rule 3

Similar pairs on one court

Put the two pairs with the closest totals on the same court, so every match is close. Court 1 gets the strongest two pairs.

Rule 4

Add a little chance

Pure balance gives the same pairs every week. A small random shuffle between players of a similar level keeps it fresh but fair.

What if the number of players is not a multiple of 4?

Each court needs 4 players. With 5, 6 or 7 players on one court, the extra players sit out and swap in after each set or each 15 minutes. The generator lists who sits out first; press Draw again for the next round so different people rest. With 6 or 7 players an Americano spreads the rests automatically. If you have more players than court places, the extra players also sit out.

Method: greedy balancing by level (sort, then pair strongest with weakest), the same idea fair-team guides describe as level-balanced random draws, for example visu.network "How to make fair random teams" (20 Oct 2026), which also advises balancing pairs rather than individuals in padel. The 1 to 7 level scale matches our level calculator. The tool is our own code.

Questions players ask

How do you make balanced teams in padel?

Sort players by level, then pair the strongest with the weakest, the second strongest with the second weakest, and so on. Put pairs with similar totals on the same court.

Can I make random padel teams without levels?

Yes. Choose Fully random, or just leave the levels out, and the generator shuffles everyone into pairs and courts.

What do I do with 5, 6 or 7 padel players?

Four play and the others sit out, then swap after each set or a fixed time. The generator lists who rests first; draw again for the next round.

What level scale should I use?

Any scale works if you use it for everyone. We use 1 to 7, the same as many club apps and our level calculator. Half levels like 3.5 are fine.

Does the generator save my players?

Yes, the list is saved on this device only, so you can reuse it next week. Nothing is sent to us.
