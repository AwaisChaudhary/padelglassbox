How much does padel cost? Work out your real cost.

Court hire, rackets, shoes, balls, lessons and memberships: see published prices for the UK, the US and Qatar, then put in your own numbers to get your cost per game, per month and per year.

Quick answer

Most of the cost of padel is court hire, and four players split it. In the UK a court is about £20 to £80 an hour (about £5 to £20 each), and in the US about $20 to $60 an hour, roughly $5 to $15 each, with busy city clubs higher. A beginner racket costs from just under £50 or about $60 to $130, and many clubs rent rackets. Playing once a week, court fees alone come to roughly £20 to £80 or $20 to $140 a month, plus balls and kit.

Last updated 2026-11-26

The monthly figure in the quick answer is our own sum: four one-hour sessions at the published per-player court prices.

How much does it cost to start playing padel?

Almost nothing

Try it first

Book a court with three friends, hire rackets at the club and wear trainers you already have. You pay your share of the court plus racket hire. Some clubs also lend community rackets and balls.

Starter kit

Once you play every week

A round beginner racket, a pair of padel or clay-court tennis shoes and a can of balls. In the US a full beginner set-up is about $100 to $250 (Padel Browser). Our cheap padel rackets guide and shoe finder help you buy once.

Regular player

Two or more games a week

Court hire becomes the big cost. Padel Browser puts a regular weekly US player at about $1,500 to $4,000 a year. This is where off-peak slots and memberships start to pay.

Why is padel so expensive?

Padel is not expensive per person when four people share the court, but a court booking can look expensive at first. The main reasons:

  • Courts cost a lot to build. A single court with glass walls usually costs tens of thousands to build; the LTA's loan scheme uses £75,000 for a floodlit outdoor court and £130,000 for a covered one (via Bounce). See how much a padel court costs.
  • Many courts are indoors or in city centres, where rent, heating and lighting add to the price.
  • Demand is high and courts are few, so peak evening and weekend slots cost the most.
  • Kit is optional at first. Rackets can be hired, so buying expensive gear early is a choice, not a must.

Reasons summarised in our own words from City AM, Bounce (23 Jul 2026) and Padel Browser (8 Jun 2026).

How can I play padel for less?

Play off-peak

Daytime and early-morning slots are usually much cheaper than evenings and weekends. In one US club example, members paid $10 off-peak against $20 peak (Padel Browser, Oct 2026).

Always split four ways

Fill the court. A singles booking of two players doubles your share. The game invite maker splits the price and writes the WhatsApp message. Run a social night with the Americano generator to fill courts with 8 or more players.

Check if a membership pays

Put the monthly fee and the member discount into the calculator. It tells you how many games a month you need before the membership is cheaper.

Don't overbuy the racket

A beginner improves just as fast with a sensible round racket. Last season's models are often discounted. Use the racket finder before you shop.

Make balls last

For social games a can lasts a few sessions. Share the cost and change them when the bounce goes: ball bounce test.

Group lessons first

Clinics cost a fraction of a private lesson per person. Compare quotes in the lesson cost checker.

Questions players ask

How much does it cost to play padel?

Mostly your share of the court. A court costs about £20 to £80 an hour in the UK and about $20 to $60 in the US, split four ways, so roughly £5 to £20 or $5 to $15 each. Busy city clubs at peak times cost more.

How much is a padel court per hour in the UK?

About £20 to £80 an hour in London, with many courts around £60 for four players (about £15 each) and off-peak slots from about £26. Prices outside London are often lower.

How much does padel cost in the US?

Court hire is usually about $20 to $60 an hour, roughly $5 to $15 each, though some city clubs charge more. A beginner set-up of racket, shoes and balls costs about $100 to $250.

Why is padel so expensive?

Courts are costly to build and many are indoors or in cities, and demand is higher than the number of courts. Split four ways, though, an hour of padel is often similar to other club sports.

How much is a padel racket for a beginner?

Basic rackets start just under £50 in the UK and about $60 to $130 in the US. You don't need one to start: most clubs hire rackets.

Is a padel club membership worth it?

Usually only if you play often. Divide the monthly fee by what you save on each court booking: that is how many games a month you need before it pays. The calculator above does this for you.

Do I need to buy padel balls?

Someone brings a can of three to each game, and it is usual to share the cost. A can lasts a few social sessions before the bounce goes.
