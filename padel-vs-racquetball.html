Padel vs racquetball vs platform tennis: the differences.

All three are racket sports where walls or screens matter, so people mix them up. Here is how court, racket, ball, serve and scoring compare, side by side, from each sport's official rules.

Quick answer

Padel is doubles on a 20 x 10 m glass court with a net and a solid racket; the ball may hit the walls after it bounces. Racquetball has no net: you hit a small rubber ball against the front wall of a closed 40 x 20 x 20 ft room, and every wall and the ceiling are in play. Platform tennis is outdoor doubles on a 44 x 20 ft court inside 12 ft wire screens, with one serve and tennis scoring.

Your background

What is the difference between padel and racquetball?

Net vs front wall

In padel two teams face each other across a net. In racquetball both players stand in the same room and take turns hitting the front wall, like squash.

When walls count

In padel the ball must bounce on your side before it hits your walls. In racquetball the ball can hit side walls, the back wall and the ceiling on the way, as long as it reaches the front wall.

Scoring

Padel uses tennis scoring and anyone can win a point. Racquetball games go to 15 and only the serving side scores.

What is the difference between padel and platform tennis?

Closest cousins

Both are doubles, use solid holed rackets or paddles, tennis scoring and let you play the ball off the walls or screens after the bounce. Players switch between them easily.

Court and walls

A padel court is bigger (20 x 10 m) with glass back walls. A platform tennis court is 44 x 20 ft inside tight wire screens on a raised deck.

Serve and season

Padel serves are underarm with 2 tries. Platform tennis allows an overarm serve, but only one in doubles. Platform tennis is mostly a winter outdoor game in the north-east US.

Questions players ask

Is padel the same as racquetball?

No. Padel is played over a net on a glass-walled court, mostly in doubles. Racquetball has no net: players hit the front wall of a closed room and every surface, including the ceiling, is in play.

Is platform tennis the same as padel?

No, but they are close. Both are doubles with solid paddles, tennis scoring and walls or screens in play after the bounce. Platform tennis is on a smaller 44 x 20 ft court inside wire screens and has only one serve in doubles.

Is padel easier than racquetball?

Most beginners find padel easier to start: the underarm serve, slower ball and doubles court make rallies come quickly. Racquetball is faster and more tiring from the first game.

How big is a racquetball court?

40 ft long, 20 ft wide and 20 ft high (about 12.2 x 6.1 x 6.1 m), under the USA Racquetball rules.

How big is a platform tennis court?

The playing court is 44 x 20 ft on a 60 x 30 ft deck, surrounded by 12 ft wire screens (APTA rules).
