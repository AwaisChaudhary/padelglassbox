How Much Does a Padel Court Cost?

A single outdoor padel court usually costs £45,000 to £80,000 in the UK and $25,000 to $65,000 in the US once it is installed. Ground work, lighting, panoramic glass and an indoor building push it higher. Below are the published ranges, what moves the price, and a free budget planner for one court or a whole club.

What changes the price of a padel court?

Standard or panoramic

A standard court has glass at the back and mesh on the sides. A panoramic court uses full glass sides with no side posts, which needs more tempered glass and heavier engineering, so it costs more.

The ground under it

The court kit costs about the same anywhere. Grading, drainage and a new concrete or asphalt base can change the total by tens of thousands, so a flat existing base (like an old tennis court) saves the most.

Indoor or outdoor

Outdoor courts are cheapest. Indoors you also pay for a building or canopy with enough clear height above the court, plus heating, ventilation and planning permission.

Lighting

LED floodlights let you sell evening slots, which is when most clubs are busiest. Budget them per court.

Turf and upkeep

Sand-filled artificial turf is the standard surface. Plan to brush and top up sand regularly and replace the turf every 5 to 10 years.

Shipping and install crew

Many court systems are made in Europe. Outside Europe, freight, import duty and a specialist install crew add to the bill.

Questions players ask

How much does a padel court cost in the UK?

Usually £45,000 to £80,000 for a single installed court, with Barbour ABI planning data putting the 2026 average at about £71,000. Projects with lighting, drainage, foundations or several courts can reach £270,000 (LTA).

How much does a padel court cost in the US?

Most 2026 estimates for a single outdoor court fall between about $25,000 and $65,000, with a typical mid-range build around $35,000 to $45,000. Site work and lighting can add a lot.

Is a panoramic padel court more expensive?

Yes. Full glass side walls need more tempered glass and stronger engineering than a standard court with mesh sides, so panoramic courts sit at the top of the price range.

Can you build a padel court on a tennis court?

Often, yes, if the old base is flat, sound and drains well. Reusing it can save most of the ground work cost; ask an installer to check it first.

How long does padel court turf last?

Plan to replace sand-filled artificial turf every 5 to 10 years, depending on how much the court is used and how well it is brushed and topped up.
