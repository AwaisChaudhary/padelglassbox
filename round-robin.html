How does a padel round robin work?

Fixed pairs, everyone meets

You keep the same partner all event. Each pair plays each other pair once (or twice). There are no knock-outs, so every team gets the same number of matches.

Round robin vs Americano

In an Americano you change partner every round and score as a single player. A round robin is for teams that want to stay together, like a club league or a box league.

Odd number of pairs

With 5, 7 or 9 pairs, one team rests each round. The tool shows who is resting, and every pair rests exactly once in each cycle.

Pick a match length

A full set gives a proper match. A race to 4 games (or a timed match) keeps a big group moving on one court booking. Agree on golden point or advantage before you start.

How the table is sorted

By default: wins (or points), then game difference, then games won, then the result between the tied pairs. Your league can use other rules, so check them first.

Turn it into a final

Many groups finish with a final between the top two pairs. Use the score keeper on a phone at the net for that match.

Questions players ask

How many matches are in a round robin with 4 teams?

Six matches over three rounds. Each pair plays three matches, one against each other pair. With two courts, each round fits on court at the same time.

How many rounds does a round robin need?

With an even number of pairs, one less than the number of pairs (6 pairs = 5 rounds). With an odd number, the same number as pairs (5 pairs = 5 rounds), because one pair rests each round.

What is the difference between a round robin and an Americano in padel?

In a round robin you keep the same partner and play every other pair. In an Americano you change partner every round and collect points as a single player.

How is a padel round robin table sorted?

Most leagues rank by matches won or league points first. Common tie-breaks are game difference, games won and then the head-to-head result. This tool uses that order; your league may use its own.

What if we have an odd number of pairs?

One pair sits out each round. Every pair rests once, so everyone still plays the same number of matches.

Can we play everyone twice?

Yes. Choose 'Twice' and the tool repeats the schedule, so each pair meets every other pair two times, like a home and away league.
