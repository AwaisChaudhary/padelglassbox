Quick answer

A simple padel league: put 4 to 6 pairs of similar level in each division, let every pair play every other pair once over a season of about 4 to 6 weeks (one match a week, booked by the pairs), score best of 3 sets with a super tie-break to 10 as the third set, give 4 points for a 2-0 win, 3 for 2-1, 2 for a 1-2 loss and 1 for a 0-2 loss, then move the top 1 or 2 pairs up a division and the bottom 1 or 2 down.

How do you run a padel league, step by step?

Step 1

Gather 8 to 12 players or more

The Padel School suggests 8 to 12 players (4 to 6 pairs) to start a league, so there are regular doubles matches. Bigger groups get more divisions.

Step 2

Group pairs by level

A league is grouped by level, unlike a mixed social. Put similar pairs together in divisions of 4 to 6. If you are unsure, ask everyone to use the level calculator.

Step 3

Set a season and a match window

Pick a season of about 4 to 6 weeks and give each round a week. Pairs book their own court (often 90 minutes) inside that week.

Step 4

Agree the format and points

Best of 3 sets with a super tie-break to 10 instead of a third set fits a 90-minute booking. Write down the points, the tie-break order and what happens if a match is not played.

Step 5

Collect results the same day

The winners send the score to the organiser or the group chat. One person updates the table, so there is one version of the truth.

Step 6

Move pairs up and down

At the end, the top pairs go up a division and the bottom pairs go down. Then the next season starts with fresh, closer divisions.

The 4-3-2-1 system rewards every pair that turns up and plays, even when they lose, which keeps lower pairs coming back. Roehampton Club's padel league uses this system. "3 for a win" is simpler and familiar from football. Both work; pick one before the season starts.

How does promotion and relegation work in a padel league?

At the end of each season, the top pairs of each division swap places with the bottom pairs of the division above. With 4 to 6 pairs per division, moving 1 pair is gentle and moving 2 mixes levels faster. Some clubs move the winner up two divisions and the last pair down two. How is a padel league table sorted when pairs are level on points?

This generator sorts by points, then set difference, then game difference, then games won. Many leagues use the head-to-head result first: Roehampton Club, for example, uses head-to-head, then the fewest sets conceded per match, then a coin toss. Use whatever your rules say and settle it before the season.

What should padel league rules include?

Rule 1

Match format

Sets to 6 with a tie-break at 6-6, and a super tie-break to 10 as the third set. Agree golden point or advantage.

Rule 2

Who books and pays

Usually the pairs book a court between them inside the match week and split the cost. Say who brings new balls.

Rule 3

Missed matches

A deadline (the end of the match week), then a walkover to the pair that was available. Points only when a match is played.

Rule 4

Rain and injuries

Allow an unfinished score to stand or be finished later. If a pair withdraws, void their results so the table stays fair.

Rule 5

Self-umpiring and honesty

Players call their own lines. Wrong results mean removal from the league. Our padel etiquette guide covers the calls.

Rule 6

Substitutes

Can a pair use a sub of the same level? Most social leagues allow one, if the opponents agree.

Questions players ask

How do you organise a padel league?

Get 8 or more players in fixed pairs, group them by level into divisions of 4 to 6 pairs, set a season of 4 to 6 weeks with one match a week, agree the format and points, collect results every week and move pairs up and down at the end.

How many pairs should be in a padel league division?

Four to six pairs works best. Four pairs is 3 matches each; six pairs is 5 matches each, which fits a 5 or 6 week season.

What is the best scoring format for a padel league match?

Best of 3 sets with a tie-break at 6-6, and a super tie-break to 10 points instead of a full third set. It fits a 90-minute court booking.

How many points for a win in a padel league?

Two common systems: 4 for a 2-0 win, 3 for 2-1, 2 for a 1-2 loss and 1 for a 0-2 loss; or simply 3 for a win, 1 for a draw and 0 for a loss.

How long should a padel league season be?

About 4 to 8 weeks. Roehampton Club runs leagues of about six weeks. Shorter seasons mean more promotion and relegation and fresher divisions.

What is the difference between a padel league and a ladder?

A league has a fixed fixture list and season, then promotion and relegation. A ladder has no end: pairs challenge the pair above them and swap places if they win.
