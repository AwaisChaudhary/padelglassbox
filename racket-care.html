Padel racket care: make your racket last

Heat, cold, damp and knocks on the glass are what wear a padel racket out. Run the two-minute health check, then follow the simple care routine below.

Quick answer

Keep your racket in a cover, in a dry place indoors, away from the sun and out of the car. Wipe off sand and sweat after every game and dry it before you put it away. Put protector tape on the top of the frame, change the overgrip when it gets slippery, and check for cracks every few weeks.

How do you look after a padel racket?

After every game

Wipe and dry

Wipe sand and dust off the faces and frame with a dry, soft cloth. If it is damp from sweat or rain, let it dry before it goes into its cover.

After every game

Cover it

Put it straight into its cover or the racket pocket of your bag, away from keys and bottles.

For stains

A little soap

Use a cloth that is only slightly damp, with a drop of mild soap. Dry it straight away. Never soak the racket or use strong cleaning products.

Every few weeks

Check for damage

Look along the frame and both faces for cracks and chips, especially at the top edge, which hits the glass and the floor most.

When worn

New overgrip

Change it when it feels slippery. Players who play a lot and sweat a lot may change it every few matches; others go a month or two.

When worn

New protector tape

Replace the tape when it peels or wears through. Old tape can trap sand and grit under it.

Does heat or cold damage a padel racket?

ConditionWhat it doesWhat to do
Heat (hot car, sun, near a heater)The foam core softens and can lose its shape, so the racket can feel different or lose power for goodNever leave it in the car or in the sun. Take it inside with you.
Cold (garage, car boot in winter)The foam core gets stiffer and harder, and cracks are more likelyStore it indoors. Let a cold racket warm up to room temperature before you play.
Damp and sweatWater can get into small cracks and the core, adding weight and changing the feelDry it after play and before you cover it. Don't store it in a damp place.
Big temperature changesRepeated hot and cold stresses the materialsKeep it somewhere with a steady temperature. A bag with a thermal pocket helps.

Most padel rackets have a core of EVA foam or softer foam inside a carbon or fibreglass shell, which is why temperature matters more than with a tennis racket. Rackets are not all the same: follow your brand's care notes if they say something different.

How do you put on protector tape?

1. Clean and dry

Remove the old tape, then wipe the top of the frame so it is clean and fully dry.

2. Start in the middle

Line the centre of the tape up with the very top of the frame and press it down there first.

3. Work outwards

Smooth the tape down one side, then the other, pulling it slightly tight so there are no bubbles or creases.

4. Mind the balance

Tape adds a little weight to the head, so the racket feels a bit more head-heavy. See how much it matters with the balance decoder.

Does protector tape add weight? Is it worth it?

QuestionShort answer
How much weight?Usually a few grams up to about 10 g. Decathlon lists its strong frame tape at 10 g; Padel Browser lists one thin protector at 6 g.
Does it change the balance?A little. Padel Browser works out that 6 g at the top of a 365 g racket moves the balance about 2 mm towards the head. Balance groups (low, medium, high) are about 1 cm apart, so most players barely feel it.
Which type?Thin clear tape keeps the feel closest to new. Thicker "strong" tape and moulded bumpers protect more and weigh more.
How long does it last?Padel Browser suggests every 2 to 3 months if you play regularly, or sooner once the frame shows through.
How do I remove it?Warm it a little (Decathlon suggests this), then peel slowly from one end.

Our view: worth it for most players, especially beginners who scrape the floor and glass. If you are fussy about balance, put the tape on the day the racket arrives so you get used to the feel from the start.

Sources, checked 28 Nov 2026: Decathlon (Kuikma) "Padel racket frame protector tape strong" product page (10 g, TPU, warm to remove); Padel Browser "Best padel racket protectors 2026" (8 Sep 2026: 6 g example, about 2 mm balance shift, replace every 2 to 3 months).

Habits that break rackets

Hitting the floor or walls

Banging the racket on the ground or the glass in frustration is the quickest way to crack the frame.

Scooping up balls

Picking balls up by scraping the face along the turf wears the face and the edge. Use your hand or foot.

Clashing with your partner

Racket clashes in the middle crack frames. Call "mine" or "yours" early (in Spanish: mía and tuya).

Leaving it in the car

A car in the sun gets very hot, and in winter very cold. Both are bad for the foam inside.

How long does a padel racket last?

There is no fixed number. It depends on how often and how hard you play, and how you store it. Instead of counting months, watch for the signs: a crack that grows, a rattle inside, dead or soft spots, or less power than when it was new. If you see them, ask the shop or the brand to look at it, and check whether it is still under warranty. Good care, protector tape and a cover all help a racket last longer.

Questions players ask

How do you look after a padel racket?

Wipe off sand and sweat after each game, dry it before you cover it, keep it in a cover in a dry place indoors, keep it out of the sun and the car, use protector tape on the frame and change the overgrip when it is slippery.

Can I leave my padel racket in the car?

It's best not to. A car gets very hot in the sun and very cold in winter. Heat softens the foam core and cold makes it stiffer and easier to crack.

Does cold weather affect a padel racket?

Yes. The foam core gets harder in the cold, so the racket feels stiffer and cracks are more likely. Let it warm up to room temperature before you play.

Is protector tape worth it on a padel racket?

Yes for most players. It protects the top of the frame from knocks on the glass and the floor, which is where cracks often start. It adds a little weight to the head.

How much weight does protector tape add to a padel racket?

Usually a few grams up to about 10 g. That moves the balance only a couple of millimetres towards the head, which most players barely feel.

How often should you replace padel protector tape?

About every 2 to 3 months if you play regularly, or as soon as it is worn through or peeling.

How do you clean a padel racket?

Use a dry, soft cloth after each game. For marks, use a cloth that is only slightly damp with a little mild soap, then dry it straight away. Never soak it or use strong cleaners.

How often should you change your padel overgrip?

When it gets slippery, shiny or torn. That can be every few matches if you play a lot and sweat a lot, or every month or two if you play less.

How do I know if my padel racket is broken?

Look for cracks in the frame or face, listen for a rattle inside, and notice dead spots, odd vibrations or a loss of power. If you find these, ask the shop or brand to check it.
