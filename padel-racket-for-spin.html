Padel racket for spin: what really makes the ball spin?

Rough faces, sand finishes and 3D textures are sold as spin. A lab test says the racket matters less than you think. Answer six questions and see where your spin is coming from, what is holding it back and whether a spin racket is worth buying.

Quick answer

Spin in padel comes mostly from your swing: brushing across the ball with a slicing, high-to-low path. A rough face helps a little on glancing, brushing contacts, but a 2022 Tennis Warehouse University lab test found most rackets give about the same spin on most shots. If you want a spin racket, pick the right shape and weight first, then choose a textured (sand or 3D) carbon face, keep it clean and play with fresh balls.

    Does a rough padel racket give more spin?

    A little, and only on some shots. In 2022 Tennis Warehouse University fired balls at clamped padel rackets at many angles and filmed the spin. When the ball hit the face at a steep angle (most rackets above about 45 degrees, almost all above 60), it gripped the face and every racket produced about the same spin, rough or smooth. Only on shallow, glancing contacts, the kind you make when you brush hard across the ball, did the rougher faces hold the ball better while smoother ones let it slide.

    Angles are measured from the face: 90 degrees is a straight-on hit, low numbers are glancing, brushing contacts. Only three rackets were tested, so treat this as a guide, not a ranking. The takeaway: your swing path makes the spin; a textured face helps keep it on brushing shots like the víbora and slice.

    Sand or silica finishGrains bonded into the faceLonger than paint, but wears where you hit most

    Every texture loses some bite as it wears, and dust and sand on the face reduce grip too. A quick wipe with a dry cloth between games helps. Care tips: padel racket care.

    How do you put more spin on the ball in padel?

    Tip 1

    Swing high to low

    For a slice or bandeja, swing from above the ball and across it, with an open face. The path makes the spin.

    Tip 2

    Brush, do not punch

    Contact the back and side of the ball and keep the face moving across it, like you are cutting it.

    Tip 3

    Head speed

    Spin needs racket speed at contact. A relaxed grip and turning your shoulders give more speed than a tight wrist.

    Tip 4

    Fresh balls

    Balls lose pressure and felt as they age, and old balls grip less. Check yours with the dead ball checker.

    Is a spin racket worth it?

    Buy for shape, weight and balance first; spin is a bonus. A beginner with a flat swing gains almost nothing from a rough face. An improving player who already slices the bandeja and plays the víbora will notice a textured carbon face more, especially when it is new. Diamond rackets give more head speed for attacking players, but have a smaller, higher sweet spot. Find your type with the control vs power test and the racket finder.

    Questions players ask

    Which padel racket gives the most spin?

    A textured (sand or 3D) carbon face gives a little more grip on brushing shots, but a lab test found most rackets give about the same spin on most shots. Your swing makes most of the spin.

    Does a rough padel racket give more spin?

    Only a little, and mainly on glancing, brushing contacts. On straight-on hits the ball grips any face, so spin is about the same.

    Does a rough padel face wear off?

    Yes. Painted grit can go smooth quickly with heavy use; sand finishes last longer and moulded 3D textures longest.

    Are rough padel rackets legal?

    Textured faces are sold by all the big brands and used on the pro tour. If you play official competition, check your federation's racket rules.

    How do I get more spin in padel?

    Swing high to low and across the ball with an open face, keep a relaxed grip for head speed, play with fresh balls and keep the face clean.

    Should a beginner buy a spin racket?

    It is not worth paying extra. Choose a forgiving round or teardrop racket with the right weight; add spin with technique later.
