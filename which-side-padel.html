Which side should I play in padel: left or right?

Answer 8 quick questions about you and your partner. Get a side, the reasons, what your job is on that side, and a drill to practise it.

Quick answer

In a pair of right-handers, the left side (revés) usually suits the player with the stronger overhead and faster movement, because middle balls and lobs down the middle are their forehand. The right side (drive) suits the more patient, consistent player who builds the point. A left-hander with a right-handed partner usually plays the right side, so both forehands cover the middle. New players should try both sides first.

    Should beginners pick a side?

    Play both at first

    In your first months, swap sides often. You learn both roles, and you find out which one feels natural. Moving well with your partner matters more than the side.

    Then settle in

    Once you play regularly with the same partner, pick sides with the quiz above and stick with them for a few weeks so you both learn the patterns.

    Still a tie?

    Play one set each way and count your unforced errors with the score keeper open. Keep the set-up with fewer errors.

    What next?

    Fix the weak shot

    Left side? Your overhead must be reliable. Learn it in bandeja vs víbora vs smash.

    Questions players ask

    Which side is better in padel, left or right?

    Neither is better. The right side (drive) builds the point with consistent, low balls. The left side (revés) usually finishes the point with overheads and takes the middle forehand. Pick the side that fits your strengths and your partner's.

    Which side should a left-handed padel player play?

    With a right-handed partner, a left-hander usually plays the right side, so both forehands and overheads cover the middle. Two left-handers mirror the usual roles.

    Who plays on the left in padel?

    In a pair of right-handers, usually the player with the more reliable overhead who moves faster, because lobs and balls down the middle come to their forehand.

    Is the right side easier in padel?

    Many beginners find the right side a good start because the job is to keep the ball in play rather than finish points. Both sides need volleys, lobs and play off the glass.

    Who takes the middle ball in padel?

    Usually the player whose forehand is in the middle: the left-side player in a pair of right-handers. Agree it before you play and call every middle ball.

    Should beginners play both sides in padel?

    Yes. Swap sides often in your first months, then settle on one side once you play regularly with the same partner.

    What is the drive and revés side in padel?

    Drive is the right side and revés is the left side, seen from your own back glass. The names come from Spanish for forehand (drive) and backhand (revés).
