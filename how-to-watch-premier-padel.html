How to watch Premier Padel: Red Bull TV, YouTube and replays.

Where to stream every round of the pro tour for free, and what to do if Red Bull TV is not live in your country.

Quick answer

In 2026, Red Bull TV streams the quarter-finals, semi-finals and finals of every Premier Padel event live and free, men's and women's, on the web and in the Red Bull TV app. The earlier rounds are free on Premier Padel's YouTube channel. Red Bull TV is not live in a few countries (including France, Belgium, Poland and Monaco), where a local broadcaster has the rights.

Last updated 2026-11-28

Which round is on which service

RoundWhere to watchCost

Quarter-finals, semi-finals, finalsRed Bull TV (web, app, smart TV)Free
Replays and highlightsRed Bull TV after the event; highlights worldwide in English and SpanishFree

Some countries have their own broadcaster for some or all rounds, so the YouTube stream can be blocked there. Red Bull's "how to watch" page for each event lists which countries get the live stream in English or Spanish.

Watching on a TV

Install the free Red Bull TV app on your smart TV, streaming stick or console, or cast it from your phone. YouTube works the same way for the early rounds.

In the USA

Red Bull TV is free with no ads, and it is the main US stream from the quarter-finals on. Early rounds are on YouTube.

What's on now

The last Major of 2026 is in Mexico: Premier Padel Mexico Major. The season ends with the Finals in Barcelona in December, then the 2027 calendar starts.

Questions players ask

Is Premier Padel free to watch?

Yes, in most countries. The early rounds are free on Premier Padel's YouTube channel, and the quarter-finals, semi-finals and finals are free on Red Bull TV.

Which Premier Padel matches are on Red Bull TV?

In 2026, Red Bull TV shows the quarter-finals, semi-finals and finals of every tour stop, for both the men's and women's draws.

Why can't I watch Premier Padel on Red Bull TV?

Red Bull TV does not have the live rights in some countries, including France, Belgium, Poland, Monaco, Andorra, Romania, Hungary, the Czech Republic and Slovakia. Highlights are still available there, and a local broadcaster shows the matches.

Can I watch Premier Padel replays?

Yes. Red Bull TV puts replays up after the event, and highlights are available worldwide in English and Spanish.

How do I watch Premier Padel on my TV?

Download the free Red Bull TV app on your smart TV, streaming device or console, or cast from your phone. Early rounds are on YouTube, which every smart TV has.
