Best padel racket for women: the specs that matter, and a quick spec picker.

Most "women's rackets" are lighter versions of a normal frame. That can be exactly right, or not. Answer five questions and get the weight, balance, shape, core and face to look for, whatever the label says.

Quick answer

Choose by level, strength and arm, not by the label. Most women do well with a racket of about 340 to 360 g, a low or medium balance and a soft core; beginners suit a round shape, improving players a teardrop, and strong advanced players can go to 360 to 370 g with a higher balance or a diamond. A women's line is usually a lighter version of the same racket, and many women play the regular version.

These bands are where Padel Market, Padel Browser and Padelful's 2026 women's guides overlap: 340 to 360 g is the common advice for beginners and anyone with arm pain, and about 365 g and up only for advanced players with solid technique.

Are women's padel rackets different?

Fact 1

Usually lighter

A women's line is most often a lighter version of an existing frame, sometimes with a lower balance or a softer core. The shape and face are often the same.

Fact 2

Not a rule

Anyone can use a women's racket, and many women, including top professionals, play the regular version. The label tells you less than the weight and balance numbers.

Fact 3

What to check

Weight in grams, balance (low, medium or high, or in mm), shape, core softness and face material. Colour and the word 'woman' do not change how it plays.

Why balance matters as much as weight

A head-heavy racket feels heavier than its weight suggests. Padel Market gives a good example: a 355 g racket with a high balance can feel heavier in the hand than a 360 g racket with a low balance. If a racket tires your wrist or shoulder by the second set, try a lower balance before you go much lighter. The numbers explained: racket weight and balance.

What grip size for smaller hands?

Most padel rackets come with one standard handle, and you change the thickness with overgrips. A grip that is too thick makes you squeeze, which tires the forearm; too thin and you also grip harder to stop it turning. Smaller hands often need just one overgrip, larger hands two. How to fit one: padel overgrip guide and how to hold a padel racket.

What is the best padel racket for women with tennis elbow?

Go lighter (about 340 to 355 g), low balance, round shape, soft core and a fibreglass or soft face, and avoid jumping to a stiff, heavy racket. Pain usually comes from the racket, technique and how much you play together, so get lasting pain checked by a doctor or physio. Check your own arm load with the padel elbow check.

Questions players ask

What weight padel racket is best for women?

Most women do well with about 340 to 360 g. Beginners and anyone with arm pain should stay at the lighter end; strong advanced players can use 360 to 370 g.

Are women's padel rackets just lighter?

Mostly. A women's line is usually a lighter version of an existing frame, sometimes with a lower balance or softer core. Many women play the regular version.

What padel racket shape is best for women?

The same as for anyone: round for beginners and control, teardrop for improving all-round players, diamond for strong attacking players.

Can men use women's padel rackets?

Yes. If the weight and balance suit you, the label does not matter.

What padel racket do female pros use?

Many top women play the same models as the men, or the women's versions of them, which are often a little lighter. Pros also have stronger technique and train every day, so their specs are not a guide for club players.

What is a good padel racket for a woman with tennis elbow?

A light (about 340 to 355 g), low-balance, round racket with a soft core and a soft face. Get lasting pain checked by a doctor or physio.
