Pickleball score keeper

Tap the side that won the rally. It keeps the score, the server number, side-outs and which side to serve from, for doubles or singles, traditional or rally scoring.

Quick answer

In doubles, call three numbers before each serve: the serving side's score, the receiving side's score, then the server number (1 or 2). The game starts at 0-0-2. Only the serving side scores in traditional scoring, games go to 11 and you must win by 2. Serve from the right when the serving side's score is even and from the left when it's odd.

How do you keep score in pickleball?

1. Only the serving side scores

In traditional (side-out) scoring, you win a point only when your side is serving and wins the rally. If the receiving side wins the rally, there's no point: the serve moves on.

2. Two servers each turn (doubles)

Both partners serve before the serve passes to the other side. When the first server loses a rally, the partner becomes server 2. When server 2 loses a rally, it's a side-out and the other side gets the serve, starting with the player on the right as server 1.

3. Why the game starts at 0-0-2

To cut the advantage of serving first, only one player on the first serving side serves at the start: the player on the right. So the game begins as if that player is already server 2, and one lost rally gives the serve away.

4. Which side to serve from

Look at the serving side's score: even (0, 2, 4…) means serve from the right, odd means the left. In doubles, the serving pair swap sides each time they win a point; the receivers never swap.

5. How a game is won

Games are usually to 11, and you must win by 2, so a game can go past 11. Some events play to 15 or 21. Matches are often one game, or best of three.

6. Singles

Call two numbers: server's score, then receiver's score. There's no server 2: lose a rally on your serve and it's a side-out. Same even-right, odd-left rule.

What is rally scoring in pickleball?

In rally scoring a point is scored on every rally, whoever served. USA Pickleball allows it for some events, with games to 11, 15 or 21, win by 2. There is no server 2, so the doubles score starts at 0-0 and is called with two numbers. The serve passes to the other side each time the receiving side wins a rally. Under the USA Pickleball rule, the game-winning point can only be won by the serving side: if the receivers win a rally at game point, they get the serve but not the point. This tool follows that rule; some leagues and guides do it differently, so check your event's rules.

Questions players ask

How do you call the score in pickleball doubles?

Three numbers: the serving side's score, the receiving side's score, then the server number, 1 or 2. For example 4-2-1.

Why does pickleball start at 0-0-2?

Only one player on the first serving side serves at the start, to reduce the advantage of serving first. So the first server is treated as server 2.

Can you score when receiving in pickleball?

Not in traditional scoring: only the serving side scores. In rally scoring, every rally scores, except that the game-winning point must be won on serve under USA Pickleball rules.

What score do you play to in pickleball?

Usually 11, win by 2. Some events play to 15 or 21.

Which side do you serve from in pickleball?

From the right when the serving side's score is even, and from the left when it's odd.

What is a side-out in pickleball?

When the serving side loses the serve to the other side: after both servers have lost a rally in doubles (or the first server at 0-0-2), or after one lost rally in singles.
