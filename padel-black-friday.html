What is the discount really worth?

What is worth buying on Black Friday?

  • Last season's racket. When shops clear old models to make room for the new year's stock, that is the deepest racket discount of the year. The 2026 version of a racket usually plays almost exactly like the 2027 one.
  • Balls by the pack. You go through them fast, so a multi-pack discount adds up. See when to change padel balls.
  • A bag or backpack if you carry more than one racket.
  • Shoes, but buy early. A discount is no use if your size sells out on day one. Check which sole you need first.

How do I avoid a fake deal?

  • Some shops keep a permanent "sale" price, so a big percentage off is not always a real drop. Compare with the price a couple of weeks earlier.
  • Pick the racket for your level first, then look for it on sale, not the other way round. Our racket finder takes a minute.
