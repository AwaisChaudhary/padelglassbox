Padel words, decoded.

Padel borrows lots of Spanish. Search any word you've heard on court.

BajadaAttacking shot taken after the ball rebounds high off the back wall.
BandejaControlled sliced overhead that keeps your team at the net.
ChiquitaSoft, low ball aimed at the net players' feet.
ContraparedHitting the ball into your own back wall so it goes over the net.
CuchillaA flat, fast overhead between a bajada and a víbora, often called the knife.
DejadaDrop shot that lands just over the net with backspin.
DriveThe player on the right side of the court (deuce side).
RevésThe player on the left side of the court, often the more attacking player.
GloboLob: a high, deep ball over the net players.
Golden pointAt 40-40, one deciding point wins the game. The receiving pair chooses the side.
Star PointA newer deuce format in the 2026 rules where play goes through advantages before a final deciding point.
MallaThe metal mesh fence around the court.
ParedWall. Any shot played off the glass.
Por 3Smash hit hard enough to bounce out over the back wall.
RemateSmash: the full-power overhead.
RuloTopspin smash aimed cross-court at the side fence.
Salida de paredPlaying the ball after it has come off the wall.
VíboraAttacking overhead with sidespin; 'viper' in Spanish.
VoleaVolley: hitting the ball before it bounces.
WillyHitting the ball backwards between your legs.
