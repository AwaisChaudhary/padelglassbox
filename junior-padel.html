Junior padel: a guide for parents, plus a starter planner.

Thinking of getting your child into padel? Tell the planner their age, experience and goal. It suggests the racket weight, ball, session length, scoring and the next step into junior competitions in the UK, the US or abroad.

Quick answer

Children can start padel young: the US federation's junior guide suggests groups from age 5 to 8, 7 to 12 and 12 to 18. For young beginners use a light junior racket, foam or low-compression balls, sessions of 30 to 60 minutes in groups of 4 to 8, and simple rally scoring before full padel scoring. In the UK, the next step is LTA Team Challenge and then the LTA Youth Tour, where juniors earn LTA ranking points.

What age can a child start padel?

There is no official minimum age. The smaller court, the walls that keep the ball in play and the underarm serve make padel easier to start than tennis. The US Padel Association's guide for clubs starting a junior programme suggests two groups to begin with, under 12 (ages 7 to 12) and under 18 (ages 12 to 18), then splitting into under 8 (ages 5 to 8) and under 14 (ages 9 to 14) as numbers grow. UK junior national events use 12U, 14U, 16U and 18U age groups.

How should a junior padel session work?

Tip 1

Safe, fun, learn

The US federation's guide puts it in that order: be safe, have fun, learn. A child who enjoys the first sessions comes back.

Tip 2

Small groups

Four to eight children per coach is the size the guide calls ideal for under-12s, so everyone gets plenty of hits.

Tip 3

Short sessions

Sixty minutes is standard. Ninety minutes suits older, keen players, but is usually too long for beginners or under-12s. First taster sessions can be 30 to 45 minutes.

Tip 4

Rallies, not queues

Avoid long lines of children waiting for one feed. Short mini-rallies of 3 to 5 shots keep everyone moving.

Tip 5

Easy rules first

Let beginners play the ball after two bounces and use simple rally scoring. Full padel scoring and the walls can come later.

Tip 6

Softer balls

Foam or low-compression balls bounce slower and lower, so small children can reach them and rallies last longer.

Junior padel competitions: where to start

UK

LTA Team Challenge

Fun team matches with friends at your local venue. The LTA describes it as a way to introduce children to padel competition.

UK

LTA Youth Tour

The first step where juniors earn LTA ranking points. Matches use a simple, fast format, so a tournament usually lasts about half a day. Find events on the LTA competitions search.

UK

Junior National Championships

The first LTA Junior Padel National Championships were held in Derby in autumn 2026, with 128 players across eight events: boys and girls 12U, 14U, 16U and 18U.

US

USPA junior events

In the US Padel Association's junior pathway, children who reach the "match play ready" stage can enter USPA junior tournaments.

World

FIP junior events

International junior tournaments are run by the International Padel Federation (FIP), and entries go through the FIP website.

Questions players ask

What age can kids start playing padel?

There is no official minimum age. The US Padel Association's junior guide includes a group for ages 5 to 8, and UK junior events start at 12U. Young children do best with a light racket, soft balls and short sessions.

Is padel good for kids?

Yes. The small court, walls that keep the ball in play and underarm serve mean children get long rallies sooner than in many racket sports, and it is played in pairs, so it is social.

What size padel racket does a child need?

Junior rackets differ mostly in weight. As a rough guide: about 260 to 300 g for ages 7 to 9, 300 to 330 g for 10 to 12 and 330 to 355 g for 13 to 14. Use our junior racket finder for your child's height.

How long should a kids' padel session be?

About 60 minutes. The US federation's guide says 90 minutes is usually too long for beginners and under-12s; first taster sessions can be 30 to 45 minutes.

Are there junior padel tournaments in the UK?

Yes. LTA Team Challenge introduces team matches, the LTA Youth Tour gives juniors their first LTA ranking points in half-day events, and there are LTA Junior Padel National Championships in 12U to 18U age groups.

What balls should kids use for padel?

Foam or low-compression balls for young beginners, because they bounce slower and lower. Move to normal padel balls once rallies last.
