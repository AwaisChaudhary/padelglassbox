How to get a DUPR rating (pickleball), and what moves it.

DUPR is the rating most pickleball clubs, leagues and tournaments use. Here is how to get rated for free, how the number moves, and a match checker that shows which team DUPR expects to win before you play.

Quick answer

Make a free DUPR account, then play one match that is submitted to DUPR (a league, ladder or tournament) or log a game yourself in the app: one result is enough to get a rating. Ratings run from 2.000 to 8.000. A DUPR coach can also give you a starting rating without matches. The number moves on how many points you win compared with what was expected, not just on wins.

How is a DUPR rating calculated?

Points against expectation

Before each match DUPR works out an expected score from everyone's rating. If your team wins more points than expected, your rating goes up, even in a loss. A 3.5 who loses 9-11 to a 4.0 can go up.

A win can drop you

If you were expected to win 11-3 but only won 11-8, your rating can go down. Beating much weaker players narrowly does not help.

Match type and recency

Club and tournament results count more than a self-posted rec game. Recent matches count more than old ones, and early results move a new rating faster.

How do I raise my DUPR rating?

Win points, not just matches

Fight for every point in a match you will lose: a close score against stronger players is what lifts the number.

Play logged matches often

Leagues, ladders and tournaments count most. More matches make your rating more stable and keep your reliability score high.

Get better, then play up

Lessons do not raise the number on their own, and nor does a new paddle. Better skills, then matches against higher-rated players, do. Our pickleball rules and score keeper help with the basics.

Is there a DUPR for padel?

DUPR is for pickleball. Padel has no single universal rating: booking apps and clubs use their own levels, and pros are ranked by FIP points. To find your padel level, try our padel level calculator, see how padel world rankings work, or compare the sports in padel vs tennis vs pickleball.

Questions players ask

How do I get a DUPR rating?

Make a free DUPR account, then play one match that is submitted to DUPR, such as a league, ladder or tournament, or log a game in the app. One submitted result gives you a rating. A DUPR coach can also assess you and give a starting rating without matches.

Is DUPR free?

Yes. Getting and keeping a DUPR rating is free. DUPR+ is an optional paid plan with extra stats and features, listed at $19.99 a year when we checked in November 2026.

What is a good DUPR rating?

DUPR runs from 2.000 to 8.000. Professionals are at the top of the scale. Most recreational players sit in the 3s and 4s, so a rating in the 4s is strong club level.

How many matches do you need for a DUPR rating?

One submitted match gives you a rating, but DUPR suggests 10 to 15 recorded matches before you fully trust it.

Why did my DUPR go down after a win?

DUPR compares the score with what was expected. If you beat lower-rated players by less than expected, your rating can drop even though you won.
