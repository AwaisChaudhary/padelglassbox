Every padel shot, explained.

What each shot is for, when to play it, and one tip to get it right. Each card shows the ball's path, with the net in the middle and the glass at the ends.

Beginner · Net

Volley

Hitting the ball before it bounces, usually near the net. Short, firm punches beat big swings.

Tip: Keep the racket up in front of your chest.
Beginner · Back

Lob (globo)

A high, deep ball over the net players. It's the shot that wins back the net when you're defending.

Tip: Aim deep, close to the back glass, so it's hard to smash.
Intermediate · Back

Contrapared

Hitting the ball into your own back wall so it rebounds over the net. A rescue shot when a lob has gone past you.

Tip: Hit up and into the glass; let the wall do the work.
Intermediate · Net

Bandeja

A controlled overhead with slice, played when the opponents lob you. It isn't meant to win the point. It keeps you at the net.

Tip: Think 'placement, not power'. Stay at the net afterwards.
Intermediate · Back

Chiquita

A soft, low ball from the back of the court that drops at the feet of the net players. It's a transition shot that lets your team move forward.

Tip: Don't try it from deep in the court; you need control.
Advanced · Net

Víbora

An attacking overhead with sidespin and more pace than the bandeja. The name means 'viper', because it skids away at an awkward angle.

Tip: Learn a solid bandeja first.
Advanced · Net

Rulo

A topspin smash aimed across the court towards the side fence, where the ball takes an unpredictable bounce. A lower-risk way to attack a short lob.

Tip: Aim cross-court at the mesh, not the glass.
Advanced · Back

Bajada

An attacking shot taken high after the ball comes off the back wall. It turns a defensive ball into an attack.

Tip: Let the ball come off the glass and meet it on the way down.
Advanced · Net

Smash (remate)

The full-power overhead. It can bounce out of the court to win the point, but a weak smash gives the ball back.

Tip: Only smash the short, high lobs.
Intermediate · Net

Dejada (drop shot)

A soft shot with backspin that dies just over the net. Best when the opponents are stuck at the back.

Tip: Disguise it like a normal volley.

Questions players ask

What is a bandeja in padel?

A controlled overhead with slice, played after a lob. Its job is to keep your team at the net, not to win the point outright.

What's the difference between a bandeja and a víbora?

Both are overheads, but the víbora adds sidespin and pace to attack, while the bandeja is safer and about placement. Learn the bandeja first.

What is a chiquita?

A soft, low ball from the back of the court that drops at the net players' feet, so your team can move forward.

What is a rulo?

A topspin smash aimed across the court at the side fence, where the ball bounces unpredictably.
