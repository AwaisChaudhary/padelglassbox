Premier Padel Mexico Major 2026 in Acapulco.

Dates, how to watch, what is at stake and who won last year. This is the last Major of the season and the last big chance to qualify for the Barcelona Finals.

Quick answer

The GNP Mexico Major runs from Monday 23 to Sunday 29 November 2026 in Acapulco, Mexico. The men's and women's finals are on Sunday 29 November. Quarter-finals to finals are live on Red Bull TV; earlier rounds are on Premier Padel's YouTube channel. Winners get 2,000 ranking points. Last year Coello and Tapia won the men's title, and Bea González and Claudia Fernández the women's.

Finals daySunday 29 November 2026
Next on the tourPremier Padel Finals, Barcelona, 10 to 13 December 2026

Acapulco is on Mexico City time (UTC-6), so evening matches there are played overnight in Europe and in the early morning in the UK.

How to watch the Mexico Major

Quarter-finals to finals

Live on Red Bull TV, which covers every 2026 Premier Padel event from the quarter-finals on, for both the men's and women's draws. The app works on phones and smart TVs.

Earlier rounds

The rounds before the quarter-finals are streamed on Premier Padel's own YouTube channel.

Not shown where you are?

Red Bull TV's live stream is not available in every country. Where it is blocked, highlights are posted worldwide in English and Spanish after the event.

It was González and Fernández's first Major as a pair. Coello and Tapia's win in Acapulco sealed their place as the year-end world No. 1 pair.

Why this week matters

Only the top eight men's and top eight women's pairs of the season play the Premier Padel Finals in Barcelona (10 to 13 December). As the last Major, Acapulco carries the most points of the closing weeks, so it can decide who makes it. Questions players ask

When is the Mexico Major 2026?

From 23 to 29 November 2026 in Acapulco. The men's and women's finals are on Sunday 29 November.

Where can I watch the Premier Padel Mexico Major?

Quarter-finals, semi-finals and finals are live on Red Bull TV. Earlier rounds are on Premier Padel's YouTube channel. Red Bull TV's live stream is not available in every country.

Who won the Mexico Major 2025?

Arturo Coello and Agustín Tapia won the men's title against Chingotto and Galán. Bea González and Claudia Fernández won the women's title against Gemma Triay and Delfina Brea.

How many points is a Premier Padel Major worth?

2,000 ranking points for the winning pair, the most of any regular tour event.

What comes after the Mexico Major?

The Premier Padel Finals in Barcelona, 10 to 13 December 2026, for the top eight pairs of the season.
