How the two formats work

Americano

You change partner every round. The aim is that you play with, and against, as many different people as possible. Nobody is knocked out.

Mexicano

Round one is random. After that, each round is made from the leaderboard, so players with similar scores meet. A common way, used here, is 1st and 4th against 2nd and 3rd on the top court, then the next four, and so on.

Points, not games

Each match is a fixed number of points, such as 24. Every point counts, even if your team loses: lose 10-14 and you still add 10 to your total. The player with the most points at the end wins.

Serving

A simple rule many clubs use: each player serves a block of points (often four in a row for 16, 24 or 32 points), then the serve passes to the other team. Agree the rule before you start.

Odd numbers of players

It works best with 4, 8, 12 or 16 players. With other numbers, some players sit out each round. This tool spreads the rests as evenly as it can, and the leaderboard shows points per match so it stays fair.

How long it takes

A 24-point match often takes around 10 to 15 minutes, and 32 points a bit longer. Seven rounds of 24 points fits a typical two-hour court booking with time for changes.

Questions players ask

What is an Americano in padel?

A social format where you change partner every round and play a set number of points per match. Each player keeps the points they win, and the highest total at the end wins.

What is the difference between Americano and Mexicano?

In Americano the partners are planned in advance so everyone mixes. In Mexicano each new round is built from the current scores, so players of a similar level end up playing together.

How many players do you need for an Americano?

At least four. Multiples of four (8, 12, 16) fill every court in every round. Other numbers work too, with a few players resting each round.

How many points per match in an Americano?

Clubs often use 16, 21, 24 or 32. Fewer points means quicker matches and more rounds in the same court time.

Do you need an app to run an Americano?

No. This page makes the draw, keeps the scores and shows the leaderboard in your browser. It saves on your device, so you can close it and come back.
