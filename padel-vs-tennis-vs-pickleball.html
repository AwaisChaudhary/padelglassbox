Padel vs tennis vs pickleball.

Three racket sports, three very different games. See the courts drawn to the same scale, compare the rules side by side, then answer five quick questions to see which one suits you best.

Quick answer

Padel is played almost always in doubles on a 20 × 10 m court closed in by glass walls you can play off, with a solid racket full of holes and a low-pressure felt ball. Tennis has the biggest court (23.77 × 10.97 m for doubles), no walls and an overhead serve. Pickleball has the smallest court (13.41 × 6.10 m, or 44 × 20 ft), a solid paddle, a plastic ball and a no-volley zone called the kitchen. Padel and pickleball are not the same sport.

What makes padel different from tennis and pickleball?

Padel

The walls change everything

In padel the ball can bounce once and then hit the glass, and you can still play it. That gives you a second chance on many shots, so rallies last longer. Read the wall and fence rules.

Tennis

Space and the serve

A tennis court is the biggest of the three, and the overhead serve is a weapon. You need more running and more technique before rallies feel easy.

Pickleball

The kitchen

Pickleball has a 7 ft (2.13 m) zone each side of the net where you can't volley. It stops players smashing from the net, so soft drop shots, called dinks, matter a lot.

Padel

Scoring like tennis

Padel uses the tennis count, but at 40-40 many games use a golden point or the newer Star Point. Keep track with our score keeper and see golden point vs Star Point.

Pickleball

Only servers score

In the traditional pickleball format, only the serving side can win points, and a game goes to 11, won by two. Some events now try rally scoring, where every rally counts.

All three

Different kit

A tennis racket has strings. A padel racket is solid foam with holes. A pickleball paddle is solid and flat. Each sport also has its own ball, so you can't swap them.

Is padel easy to learn if you play tennis or pickleball?

From tennis

Your volleys and footwork help a lot. The hard part is letting the ball pass you and come off the glass instead of hitting it early. Shorten your swing: big tennis swings send the ball into the back glass. Start with the shot library.

From pickleball

You already know the underhand serve and how to win at the net. In padel the serve must bounce first, there is no kitchen, and lobs over the net players are a main weapon. Learn where to stand as a pair.

Brand new

Padel is a good first racket sport: the court is small and the walls keep the ball in. Read the first match checklist and find a racket that suits you.

Questions players ask

What is the difference between padel and pickleball?

Padel is played on a 20 × 10 m court with glass walls you can use, with a solid holed racket and a felt ball, almost always in doubles. Pickleball is played on a smaller 44 × 20 ft court with no walls, a solid paddle and a plastic ball, and it has a non-volley zone called the kitchen.

Is padel easier than tennis?

For most beginners, yes. The court is smaller, the serve is underhand and the walls keep the ball in play, so rallies come much sooner than in tennis.

Is padel or pickleball easier to learn?

Both are quick to learn. Pickleball is often called the easiest of the three to pick up, and padel the easiest way into a racket sport with walls. Many people play both.

Which court is bigger, padel or pickleball?

Padel. A padel court is 20 × 10 m (200 m²). A pickleball court is 13.41 × 6.10 m (about 82 m²), so it is less than half the size.

Is a padel court smaller than a tennis court?

Yes. A padel court is 20 × 10 m. A tennis doubles court is 23.77 × 10.97 m, so padel covers about three quarters of the area, and the walls make it feel even smaller.

Is padel scored like tennis?

Yes: 15, 30, 40 and game, with sets to six. Many padel games use a golden point at 40-40, and the pro tours use Star Point from 2026.

Are padel and pickleball the same thing?

No. They are different sports. Padel has glass walls, a felt ball and a racket with holes, on a 20 × 10 m court. Pickleball has no walls, a plastic ball with holes and a solid paddle, on a 44 × 20 ft court with a kitchen zone.

Can you use a tennis racket or pickleball paddle for padel?

No. Padel rackets are solid with holes and no strings, and the FIP rules set their size. Each sport has its own racket and ball.

Which is the best workout: padel, tennis or pickleball?

Tennis usually means the most running. Padel is lots of short, quick movements and bending. Pickleball is the lowest impact of the three. Any of them is good exercise if you play regularly.
