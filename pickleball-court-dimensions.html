Pickleball court dimensions

Every line on a regulation pickleball court, the kitchen, the net and how much space to leave around it, in feet and metres.

Quick answer

A pickleball court is 20 ft wide and 44 ft long (6.10 × 13.41 m), the same for singles and doubles. The non-volley zone (the kitchen) runs 7 ft back from the net on each side. The net is 36 in high at the sidelines and 34 in in the middle. Allow a total playing area of at least 30 × 60 ft; 34 × 64 ft is recommended.

How many pickleball courts fit on a tennis court?

Four, if the surface around the tennis court is used: a tennis court with its run-off is about 60 × 120 ft, which fits four 30 × 60 ft pickleball areas. With only the tennis lines (36 × 78 ft) most clubs paint one or two pickleball courts over it.

Can I mark a pickleball court on a badminton court?

Yes. A badminton doubles court is 20 × 44 ft too, so only the kitchen line needs adding: 7 ft from the net, where badminton's short service line sits at about 6 ft 6 in. Pickleball's net is lower, so you need a pickleball net.

Marking out a court at home

Measure the 20 × 44 ft rectangle, check it's square by measuring both diagonals (each should be about 48 ft 4 in), then add the kitchen lines 7 ft from the centre on each side and the centre lines from the kitchen to the baselines. Chalk or tape works for temporary courts.

Questions players ask

What are the dimensions of a pickleball court?

20 ft wide by 44 ft long (6.10 × 13.41 m), for both singles and doubles.

How big is the kitchen in pickleball?

The non-volley zone runs 7 ft from the net on each side, across the full 20 ft width.

How high is a pickleball net?

36 in at the sidelines and 34 in in the centre.

How much space do I need for a pickleball court?

At least 30 × 60 ft in total; 34 × 64 ft is recommended so players can move around the court.

Is a pickleball court the same size as a badminton court?

Almost exactly: a badminton doubles court is 13.40 × 6.10 m, a pickleball court 13.41 × 6.10 m. The kitchen line and net height differ.
