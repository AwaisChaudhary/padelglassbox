What is my padel level?

Answer seven quick questions about your game. You get an estimated level from 1 to 7, what it means, and the one thing to work on next. Free and private: your answers stay on this device.

Quick answer

Padel levels run from 0 or 1 (complete beginner) to 7 (professional). Most club players are between 2 and 4.5: about 1 to 2.5 if you are new, 3 to 3.5 if you can rally and use the back glass, 4 to 4.5 if you play a reliable bandeja and think about tactics, and 5 or more if you play regular tournaments. On Playtomic, your level then moves up or down after each competitive match.

What do padel levels 1 to 7 mean?

A general guide. Each club or app words it a little differently.

1 to 2.5

Beginner

You are learning the serve, the scoring and how the ball comes off the glass. Rallies are short. Most new players move up quickly here.

3 to 3.5

Improver

You can rally, use the back glass on easy balls and move with your partner. Many tennis and squash players start around here.

4 to 4.5

Intermediate

You control the court, play a bandeja, defend the corners and think about tactics. This is a strong club player.

5 to 5.5

Advanced

You play tournaments, build points on purpose and rarely give away easy errors. Ranked players often sit at 5.5 and up.

6 to 7

Elite

Semi-professional and professional players. Pros are rated by the FIP world ranking instead: see the padel rankings and how pro points work.

Why does your padel level matter?

Better matches

Games are most fun when all four players are close in level. Booking apps use levels to suggest open matches for you.

Different scales

Not every system is the same. Playtomic uses 0 to 7 and moves your level after each rated match. The LTA in the UK uses 1.0 to 7.0. A 3 in one place may not be a 3 in another.

Your real level comes from results

A quiz gives you a starting point. Rated matches, or a coach watching you play, give a truer number over time.

Running a social night?

Ask players for a rough level, then use our Mexicano generator: it pairs people with similar scores as the night goes on.

How does the Playtomic level work?

From Playtomic's help pages, checked 2026-11-25. Apps change their rules, so check the app if something looks different.

Start

One questionnaire

When you join, a short set of questions about your experience gives your starting level on a 0 to 7 scale. You can only do it once.

Moves

Results against expectations

After a competitive match, the app compares each team's average level with the result. Beat a stronger pair and you go up more; lose to a weaker pair and you go down more. Even a draw can move levels.

Reliability

Big jumps at first

Each level has a reliability figure. When it is low, your level moves a lot after each match. As you play more competitive matches it rises and the changes get smaller. You can't change it yourself.

Casual

Not every match counts

Only competitive matches with an accepted, valid score count. Pick "casual" when you book if you just want a friendly game that won't touch your level.

Fix it

Levelling session

Certified clubs can run a levelling session with a coach, which sets your level and puts its reliability at 50%. Useful if your starting level was far off.

Fix it

10-Match Challenge

If your reliability is 85% or more and you think your level is wrong, you can opt in to 10 competitive matches where changes count 2, 3 or 4 times. You can't stop once you start, and you can repeat it after 3 months.

Questions players ask

What padel level am I?

Most people who play for fun sit between 1 and 4 on a 1 to 7 scale. Beginners are 1 to 2.5, improvers 3 to 3.5, strong club players 4 to 4.5, and tournament players 5 and up. Answer the questions on this page for your estimate.

What is a good padel level?

There is no bad level. As a rough guide, a 3 can enjoy long rallies, a 4 is a solid club player and a 5 plays regular tournaments.

How does the Playtomic level work?

Playtomic uses a 0 to 7 scale. You start with a level from a short questionnaire, then it goes up or down after each competitive match, based on the result and the average level of each team. Beating stronger players moves you up more.

Why did my Playtomic level change so much?

When your level's reliability is low, usually because you haven't played many competitive matches, each result moves it a lot. The changes get smaller as reliability rises.

Do friendly matches change my Playtomic level?

Not if you choose a casual match. Only competitive matches with an accepted, valid score change your level.

Can I change my Playtomic level?

You can't edit reliability yourself. Options are a levelling session at a certified club, which sets your level, or the 10-Match Challenge if your reliability is 85% or higher.

Is this calculator an official rating?

No. It's a free estimate to help you pick games and know what to practise. Clubs, apps and federations each run their own rating systems.

What level is a tennis player starting padel?

Many good tennis or squash players start around 3 to 3.5, because they already hit the ball well. Learning the glass and the net game usually takes a few months.

How do I move up a level?

Fix the weakest part of your game first, as it costs you the most points. For most players that means getting comfortable with the back glass and learning the bandeja.
